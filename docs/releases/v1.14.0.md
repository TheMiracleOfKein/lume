# Lume v1.14.0

Translated novels in English. Royal Road and Scribble Hub carry mostly original web fiction; this release adds two large sites with Chinese, Korean and Japanese novels translated into English, light novels included.

## New

- **NovelFull** (novelfull.com) and **NOVGO** (novgo.net, the former AllNovelFull) in Ranobe mode, full and ranobe builds. Examples: Overlord (LN), Classroom of the Elite, Re:Zero, Lord of the Mysteries, Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint.
- Catalog (popular and latest), search, title details with author, genres, status and score, and the whole chapter list in reading order, even for titles with thousands of chapters.
- **Filters**: a list (popular, latest updates, trending or completed) and a genre. The sites search by title only, so a search ignores the filters, and a genre ignores the list choice.
- Chapters open in the text reader without the sites' ads or their "report chapter" line, and download like other novel chapters. Read aloud, translation and the codex work on them as on Royal Road.
- Links to a title or a chapter on either site, opened from the browser or shared to Lume, open the title.

## Not yet

- Bigger light-novel sites (NovelBin, NovelFire, FreeWebNovel, Ranobes) blocked plain requests with a Cloudflare check when they were tried, so they are not in this release.
