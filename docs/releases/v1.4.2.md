# Lume v1.4.2

Reader settings can be reset to their defaults in one tap.

## New

- **Reset to defaults** in the reader's settings sheet:
  - text reader: text size, line spacing, font, margins, paragraph spacing, justification, colours and page-by-page mode go back to the defaults, at once, in the open chapter;
  - manga reader: the reader settings go back to the defaults, and the reading mode, theme and page gap saved for the open title are dropped, so it follows the defaults again.
- The same button at the bottom of **Settings → Reader**, for the manga and the text reader.
- Every reset asks first, and only the reader settings change. Library, progress, sources and the app's own look stay as they are.

## Updating

Install it over v1.4.1 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads and sign-ins are kept. No database change.

## For developers

- `ResetReaderSettingsButton` in `core/ui` (button and confirmation dialog), used by both reader sheets and both Settings pages.
- `NovelReaderViewModel.resetSettings()` saves `NovelReaderSettings()`. `ReaderViewModel.resetSettings()` saves `ReaderSettings()` and sets the title's `readerMode`, `readerTheme` and `gap` to null, keeping its other title prefs. Both have unit tests.
