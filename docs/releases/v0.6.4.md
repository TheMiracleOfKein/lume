# Lume v0.6.4

Similar titles: finished something you liked? The title screen now shows what other readers recommend, and one tap looks for it on your sources.

## Highlights

- **Similar titles.** At the bottom of a title's screen, a row of covers shows titles that AniList users recommend for it. No account is needed.
- **One tap to read it.** Tap a cover and Lume opens the search on all your sources with that title's name, so you can open it from whichever source has it.
- **MyAnimeList too.** If you are signed in to MyAnimeList and AniList doesn't know a title (or has no recommendations for it), Lume asks MyAnimeList instead. The row says where the recommendations come from.
- **Linked titles match exactly.** A title linked to AniList or MyAnimeList in **Trackers** uses that link. Other titles are found by their name and their other names; when nothing is clearly the same title, the row stays hidden rather than showing another title's recommendations.
- **18+ follows your choice.** Recommendations marked adult on AniList are only shown when **Include 18+ sources in global search** is on.
- **You can turn it off.** **Settings → Sources and search → Similar titles.** Settings search finds it too ("recommendations", "рекомендации").

## Privacy

To show similar titles, Lume sends the title's name (or its AniList / MyAnimeList ID when the title is linked) to AniList, and to MyAnimeList when you are signed in there. Nothing else about you or your library is sent. With the setting off, no request is made. [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md) lists it.

## Updating

Install it over v0.6.3 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## Known limits

- Recommendations are kept in memory while Lume runs; after a restart they are asked for again the first time you open a title.
- AniList knows titles by their English, romaji and native names and many Russian ones. A title only known by a name AniList doesn't have shows no row.
- The search uses the recommended title's English name (or romaji). Sources that only list a title under another name may not find it.

## For developers

- `data/similar/SimilarTitlesRepository` asks AniList's public GraphQL API (`Page.media(search)` or `Media(id)` with `recommendations(sort: RATING_DESC)`) in one request per name, and `MyAnimeListTracker.recommendations()` (`GET /v2/manga/{id}?fields=recommendations`) as the second option. Results are cached per title in memory (100 titles); failed requests aren't cached.
- Parsing, matching (`pickSimilarCandidate`: an equal name, else word overlap of at least 0.75) and cleaning (`cleanSimilar`: no duplicates, not the title itself, adult only when allowed, at most 15) are unit-tested in `SimilarTitlesTest`.
- New route `search/all?q=…` (`Routes.searchAll`) opens global search with a query and starts it once.
- New setting `similar_titles` (default on), registered in `SettingsCatalog`.
- `Database.kt` wasn't touched, so its split moves on to a later release.
