# Lume v0.7.0

Sync between devices, and a handful of reader controls people asked for: read on the phone, continue on the tablet, without an account.

## Highlights

- **Sync between devices.** Settings → Sync keeps your library the same on your phone and tablet: titles and categories, the exact spot in every chapter, read marks, page bookmarks, linked sources, tracker links, per-title reader settings and source packs.
- **No Lume account, no Lume server.** Pick where the sync files live:
  - **a folder** that another app keeps the same on your devices, for example Syncthing or a cloud drive app;
  - **a WebDAV server**: Nextcloud, ownCloud, a NAS or any WebDAV folder.
- **No write conflicts.** Each device writes only its own small file and reads the others'. The newest change wins. For a chapter, the furthest spot wins, unless you marked it unread. "Continue" goes to the title you read last, even if that device synced hours later.
- **Removals sync too.** Remove a title, a bookmark or a read mark on one device and it goes on the others. A fresh install or cleared app data never removes anything elsewhere.
- **Optional passphrase.** With a passphrase the sync files are encrypted on the phone (AES-256-GCM) before they reach the folder or the server. Every device needs the same one.
- **Sync now, in the background, and at start.** Sync every 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours, or only by hand. The status line shows the last sync, how many other devices it saw, and what went wrong if it failed.
- **Page slider.** Drag along the bottom bar to any page of the chapter; it runs right to left in right-to-left mode.
- **Orientation lock.** Auto, portrait or landscape while reading.
- **Webtoon auto-scroll.** The play button in the bottom bar scrolls on by itself; the speed is in the reader settings. Dragging the page stops it.
- **Tap zones in webtoon mode** (off by default): the top third scrolls back, the bottom third scrolls on.
- **Colour filters and e-ink mode.** Sepia, grey or warm (less blue light) pages. E-ink mode shows grey, high-contrast pages and turns pages without animation.
- **Own brightness** while reading, separate from the system's.
- **Share or save a page.** The new page button in the reader's top bar shares the page (with a link to its chapter) or saves it to Pictures/Lume.

## Privacy

Sync through a folder sends nothing by itself; the app that keeps the folder in step does that. Sync through WebDAV sends your sync file and sign-in only to the server you entered. Sign-ins, tracker tokens, downloads and app settings never go into a sync file. The sync settings, the WebDAV password and the passphrase stay on the device and out of backups. [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md) has the details.

## Updating

Install it over v0.6.6 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change. Sync is off until you turn it on.

To start syncing: turn on Sync on the device with your library first, then on the others with the same folder or server address (and the same passphrase). The libraries are combined; nothing is removed by turning sync on.

## Known limits

- Google Drive isn't a sync place yet: it needs an OAuth client. A cloud drive app that syncs a folder works through "Folder".
- Downloads don't sync. Sending downloaded chapters to a nearby phone is planned for a v0.7.x release.
- App settings (theme, reader defaults…) stay per device.
- A device that stays offline for more than 180 days may bring back titles that were removed elsewhere meanwhile.
- Saving a page on Android 9 and older asks where to save it.

## For developers

- `data/sync`: `SyncMerge` (pure merge rules: `stamp`, `merge`, `changes`, `looksWiped`), `SyncCodec` (gzip, optional AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2, header `LUMESYNC1`), `FolderSyncBackend` (SAF tree) and `WebDavSyncBackend` (PROPFIND, GET, PUT, MKCOL, no site cookies), `SyncRepository` (reads Room into records, applies changes in one transaction), `SyncWorker`.
- Sync records are keyed per collection; categories travel by name, because their ids differ between devices. The device id and the last merged state live in `noBackupFilesDir`, so a restored or transferred install gets an id of its own.
- `SyncSettings` and `SyncStatus` live in the private DataStore; `sync_*` keys are never exported.
- `ReaderSettings` gains `orientation`, `colorFilter`, `eInk`, `brightness`, `autoScrollSpeed` and `webtoonTapZones`. `ReaderEffects.kt` holds the colour matrices, the window effects and page saving.
