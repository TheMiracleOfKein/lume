# Lume v0.7.15

UI fixes, part two: text that fits at large font sizes, buttons and chips that are easier to tap, better TalkBack support, consistent icons, proper Russian plurals, and clearer empty, error and confirmation states.

## Fixed

- **Text that fits.** Long titles, source names, chapter names and download names end with "…" instead of being cut off or pushing buttons off screen. Rows on Home, Updates and in the reader settings grow with large font sizes. The chips at the top of the menu and the buttons in the update dialog wrap onto a new line when they don't fit, for example in Russian.
- **Easier to tap.** Chips (settings choices, library categories and filters, genres, chapter teams, filter chips), the Continue button on covers and the small source buttons have a full-size touch area. The visible size is the same.
- **TalkBack and accessibility.**
  - Buttons that had no label now have one: closing the reader settings, and logging in to or out of a source.
  - A row with a switch or checkbox is one target: tap anywhere on it.
  - Selected tabs, chips and reader modes are announced as selected. The nav bar shows a pill behind the selected tab, and the centre button reads "Home".
  - Headings, live status messages, long-press actions and the library status of titles are announced.
  - Long-press an icon in a selection bar to see what it does.
- **Icons.** Adding a title to the library uses the heart everywhere. In the reader, the page menu uses ⋮ and settings has a single icon. The reader's previous and next chapter icons flip in right-to-left languages. Trackers in Settings have their own icon.
- **Reader sheets.** The reader settings and bookmarks sheets have the same header, a drag handle and a close button. The gap setting shows only in Webtoon mode. The tap zones setting shows only in page modes.
- **Russian.** Reader modes are translated. Counts like "3 of 10" use the right plural forms. Scores use your locale's number format.
- **Home screen widget.** It can be resized smaller. It shows a book icon while a cover loads, and on Android 12 and newer its corners match the system.

## Clearer states

- A title with no chapters says "No chapters yet" and offers Retry, instead of "No chapters match filters".
- An empty source feed says so instead of showing a blank screen. If loading more titles fails, the list ends with a Retry row.
- On Home, a failed refresh shows a notice with Retry. Search shows a hint before your first search. A source that returns a broken response shows a readable message.
- An empty library now says "Your library is empty".
- When saving to a tracker fails, the error shows under the form and your edits are kept.

## Safer

- Clearing cookies, clearing the image cache and restoring downloads now ask first. Signing out of a tracker asks first too.
- A deleted page bookmark can be brought back with Undo.
- Password fields have a show/hide button. The keyboard's Next and Done keys move between fields and submit.
- A category can't be saved with a blank name.

## Updating

Install it over v0.7.14 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## For developers

- New shared components in `components/Widgets.kt`: `SheetHeader`, `TooltipIconButton`, `ConfirmDialog` and `PasswordField`.
- `SwitchRow`, `CheckRow`, `ChoiceChip` and `SegmentTab` carry their own `toggleable` / `selectable` semantics. Their child controls get a `null` callback.
- `NavSlot` draws a `selection` pill. `HomeSlot` is shared by the bottom bar and the rail. `LumeLogo` takes `ringed` and `contentDescription`.
- `ReaderMode.label` is now `labelRes`.
- `CatalogFeed.moreError` keeps load-more errors apart from the first load. `catalogFeedIsEmpty` has a JVM test.
- Widget radii come from `widget_dimens.xml`, with system radius dimens on v31.
