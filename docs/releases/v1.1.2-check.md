## Problems found

### Serious

None found. The green stripes did not appear on any of the pages checked (Vol. 37 Ch. 288 pages 1 to 3, Vol. 30 Ch. 200 page 1).

### Minor

1. **"Поднятие уровня в одиночку: Рагнарёк" (MangaLib) does not open.** Steps: MangaLib, search "One Punch" or "Solo", tap the "Поднятие уровня в одиночку: Рагнарёк" card in the MangaLib results. The title screen shows "Not found on api.cdnlibs.org (HTTP 404). The title or chapter may have been removed." Retry gives the same result. The change does not touch title loading, so the cause is probably the source's record for this title (search lists it, the title lookup returns 404). Not checked on the site, so it could not be confirmed as a site-side removal. Worth a look before release, because it was the title suggested for the paid-chapter check.
2. **MangaDex chapter lists are empty.** "Solo Leveling: Arise" shows Chapter list (0) and "No chapters yet"; "Na Honjaman Level-Up" shows Chapter list (0) and "No chapters yet". Retry did not change this. Logcat shows no error. MangaDex is not part of the LibSocial change, so this is probably not from v1.1.2, but it was not compared with v1.1.1.
3. **Possible gap in Ванпанчмен's list.** Vol. 29 Ch. 194 is missing between Ch. 195 and Ch. 193. This may be a paid chapter that is now hidden (expected from the fix), or a gap on the source. The site was not compared, so this is only a note. **Checked after the check:** the site's chapter API has no Vol. 29 Ch. 194 either (it goes from 193 to 195), so this is a gap on the source, not a hidden chapter.
4. **Emulator uninstall failed.** `adb uninstall com.lume.app` returned DELETE_FAILED_INTERNAL_ERROR. `install -r` succeeded, so the device was not clean, but the new build ran. This is an emulator issue, not an app issue.
