## Failures

### F1. Light theme: uppercase section headers are low contrast (T5/T6)
- Severity: visual only, not release-blocking. No crash, navigation or data impact.
- Repro:
  1. Menu → Settings → Appearance → tap **Light**.
  2. Back → tap **Sync**.
  3. The "OPTIONS" and "STATUS" headers are bright orange on the light grey background (about 2:1 contrast).
  4. Menu → Settings → Appearance: the "LANGUAGE" header has the same issue.
- Expected: the header accent in Light is the darker orange already used for TextButtons (for example "Cancel" in the WebDAV dialog and the reader settings label), matching the Dark theme's contrast.
- Screenshots: 25-sync-light.png, 02-settings-appearance-current.png.

### Minor observations (not in the table)
- Dark theme: the centre "Lume" button in the bottom nav loses its dark pill against the dark nav bar; only the orange logo shows (20-library-filters-restored-dark.png, 23-updates-after-update-now.png). Light is fine.
- The uninstall before install returned `DELETE_FAILED_INTERNAL_ERROR`. `install -r` succeeded, so the app kept its previous data. This was not a clean-install test.
