# Lume v0.9.12

Cards under **Continue reading** on Home show the title's cover again.

## Fixed

- **Covers in Continue reading.** Some cards showed a cut-out of the page you stopped at instead of the cover, which made titles hard to recognise. Every card now shows the cover. The progress bar, the chapter name and long-press **Remove** are unchanged.

## Updating

Install it over v0.9.11 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads and sign-ins are kept. There are no database changes. Backups from every earlier version still restore.

## For developers

- `HomeScreen.ContinueCard` draws `CoverImage` instead of `PageSpotImage` with a cover fallback. The saved page spot (`pageImageUrl`, `pageOffset`) is still recorded and used elsewhere.
- Not checked on a device: released without the emulator gate and smoke test, at the owner's request.
