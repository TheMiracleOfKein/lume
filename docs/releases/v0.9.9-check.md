## Notes (not bugs as far as checked)
- Reading statistics showed 0 chapters after reading ~9 of 97 pages of Berserk ch. 0.01 in the app, with 9 pages and 2 minutes counted. This matches the code: the reader marks a chapter read only when its ending is on screen (ReaderViewModel.onPosition, `finished`). The chapter count is therefore correct for a partial read. A positive chapter count was not verified, because I did not finish a chapter.
- MangaBuff now keeps a "Failed ... not available in your region" line on its Sources row after the log is cleared. This is the source's own health status, not the log.
- The reinstall restored the library from the device's backup (titles like Overgeared were present on a "fresh" install). The empty state of Reading statistics on a truly fresh install was NOT checked.

## Build note for the caller
Overriding JAVA_HOME in the Bash session with a POSIX path (/d/...) breaks gradlew.bat, which runs under cmd. Use the session's existing Windows-style JAVA_HOME. My first build attempt failed because of this, and the following install was the stale APK until I rebuilt.
