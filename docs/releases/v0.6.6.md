# Lume v0.6.6

Source filters and browse sections: search a source by its genres and tags, and browse more than its popular and latest lists.

## Highlights

- **The site's own filters.** The filter button in Search now loads the source's own genres, tags, types, statuses and formats: MangaDex (all its tags), MangaLib, YaoiLib and HentaiLib, ReManga, Madara packs, Asura Scans, HiveToons and Vortex Scans, and WeebCentral packs.
- **Include or exclude.** Where the site allows it, tap a genre once to include it (+) and again to exclude it (struck through). "Has all / has any" decides what several genres mean.
- **Long lists stay short.** Groups with many entries start folded, show what you picked, and have a field to find an entry.
- **Filters with search text.** On MangaDex, the Lib sites, Madara packs, HiveToons and WeebCentral, filters and the text you type work together. ReManga and Asura can only filter their catalogue; the panel tells you to clear the search field there.
- **Browse sections.** The catalogue's Popular tab has a row of chips per source: "New titles", "Top rated", "Completed", "Most viewed", "Manhwa" and so on.
- **More about each title.** The title screen shows the author and artist, plus the type, year and the site's score ("Manhwa · 2018 · ★ 9.3").
- **Better matches across sources.** MangaLib, ReManga, Madara and Asura now pass along a title's other names, so "Found for you" finds it on more of your sources.

## Privacy

The filter lists come from the source itself, the first time you open the filter panel for that source. For MangaDex, the title screen also asks MangaDex for the title's score. No other service is contacted.

## Updating

Install it over v0.6.5 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## Known limits

- MangaBuff and MangaKatana have no filters yet.
- ReManga shows adult genres (for example "Яой") only to signed-in users, so filtering by them finds nothing without an account.
- Filter lists are kept while Lume runs. If a site doesn't answer, the panel shows the basic filters and tries again the next time you open it.
- MangaLib, YaoiLib and HentaiLib titles have no description at the moment: the site no longer sends it to visitors who aren't signed in. This was already the case in v0.6.5.

## For developers

- `CatalogSource.loadFilters()` returns the site's filter lists (default: `getFilters()`), cached per engine by `SiteFilters`; `browseSections()` lists `BrowseSection`s, which are searches without text with preset filters; `filtersWithQuery` says whether text and filters combine.
- `FilterList.carryOver()` keeps the user's choices when longer lists arrive; `choose()` and `include()` build sections. Filter ids inside site groups are `<group>:<site id>`.
- `TitleDetails` gains `year` and `score` (0–10). The live source check also loads each source's filters, searches with one genre and opens every browse section.
