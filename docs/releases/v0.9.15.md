# Lume v0.9.15

A fix release: the filter sheet no longer bounces up and down on its own.

## Fixed

- **Filter sheet bouncing.** On sources with many filters (catalogue search and the search screen), the filter sheet could keep moving up and down by itself, even with no keyboard open. The sheet now stops below the status bar, and long filter lists scroll inside it.

## Updating

Install it over v0.9.14 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads and sign-ins are kept. No database change.

## For developers

- Cause: `ModalBottomSheet` (Material3 1.4) pads its content by the part of the status bar the sheet overlaps (`consumeWindowInsets(top = offset)` + `safeDrawing` top). Content tall enough to reach the status bar changed height with the sheet's own offset, so the Expanded anchor moved with every frame of the settle animation and the sheet never came to rest.
- Fix: `FilterPanel` is capped with `heightIn(max = window height − status bar − safe-drawing bottom − 72dp)`, so the sheet's offset stays below the status bar and that padding stays zero. The v0.9.14 `dropFocusOnDrag` stays.
- Not checked on a device: released without the emulator gate or a smoke test, at the owner's request.
