# Lume v1.17.0

Small things that get in the way every day: a button out of reach, a line that jumps, search results in the wrong order and a Google password asked for again.

## Changed

- **Read / Continue at the bottom.** On a title's page the button is now a wide bar at the bottom of the screen, where the thumb reaches it. The rest of the page scrolls above it.
- **Global search puts the best match first.** Rows keep their order while sources answer, so nothing moves under your finger. Once every source has answered, the sources whose titles match your search by name move to the top. A Russian search no longer leaves the source with the exact Russian title below one that only has English names. Sources that found nothing or failed stay at the bottom.

## Fixed

- **The novel reader's status line no longer jumps.** Closing the menu with a tap showed the time, progress and battery line above the leaving bottom bar for a moment, before it dropped into place. It now appears at the bottom straight away. The recap offer and the read-aloud panel moved the same way and are fixed too.
- **Google sign-in on Lib sites is remembered.** When the MangaLib-family login page found an old site session, it cleared every cookie, the Google sign-in included, so **Sign in with Google** asked for the Google password again. The Google account now stays signed in.

## Details

- Signing in through the phone's browser with its saved Google accounts was tried and left out: lib.social only returns to its own pages after a sign-in, so the browser can't hand the account back to Lume.
- No database change and no new permissions. Backups and sync files are the same as in v1.16.0.
- The Lume Translate add-on is unchanged.
