# Lume v0.9.4

A fix for blurry long pages in webtoon mode.

## Fixed

- **Long webtoon strips are sharp again.** A page made of many frames in one long image was shrunk to fit a height limit and then stretched to the screen width, so it looked blurry and pixelated in webtoon mode. Such pages are drawn from the original image again, in slices at full quality, as before v0.9.1.

## Updating

Install it over v0.9.3 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## For developers

- Since the move to Coil 3, a strip shown at full width is decoded into at most screen width × 4096 px, and Coil fits the whole image inside that box. The decoded height never went over 4096 px, so the check in `ReaderPageImage` that switches a page to `TiledPage` never fired. The new `isTallAtWidth` in `TallPageTiles.kt` judges the page by its shape at screen width instead. `openTallSource` still makes the final call from the original file.
- New cases in `TallPageTilesTest`.
- Device check: [v0.9.4-check.md](v0.9.4-check.md).
