# Lume v0.6.0

One title can now live on several sources at once. Lume finds a title from your library on your other sources, fills the gaps in its chapter list from them, reads a chapter from another source when its own fails, and tells you which source published a new chapter first. A title can move to another source in one tap, with everything attached to it. A home-screen widget and launcher shortcuts take you back to where you stopped.

## Highlights

- **Also on …** When you add a title to the library, Lume looks for it on your other sources and suggests the ones that have it under the same name. Link them from the card on the title screen.
- **One chapter list from several sources.** Chapters the main source doesn't have come from the linked sources, in the order you choose. Each row says where its chapter comes from.
- **Never stuck.** When a chapter doesn't load from its source (a site check, a region block, a removed chapter), the reader opens another source's copy and says so. Your place in the chapter and its read mark stay.
- **Move a title to another source.** Progress, the spot in each chapter, read chapters, page bookmarks, categories, tracker links and downloads move with it.
- **Continue from the home screen.** A widget with your last three titles, and a launcher shortcut for each.

## One title, many sources

- **Title → Sources** shows the main source, the linked sources and the suggestions. **Find on other sources** searches every enabled source by all the title's names and lists what it finds, marked "Same title" or "Maybe the same title".
- **Link** adds a source. Its chapters join the list; a chapter both sources have comes from the main source. Change the order of linked sources with the arrows.
- **Make main** makes a linked source the title's main source. Everything moves with the title and the old main source stays linked, so nothing is lost.
- **Move here** (on a search result) moves the title to that source and unlinks the old one.
- **Unlink** removes a source. Read marks, spots, page bookmarks and downloads of its chapters move to another source's copy of the same chapter where there is one; downloads of chapters no other source has are deleted.
- **Chapter list:** a source badge on every row, **One per chapter** or **Show all copies**. A chapter read on one source counts as read on all.
- **Updates:** library checks ask every linked source. The copy found first is the new chapter, and the notification and the Updates screen say "first on MangaDex".
- **Settings → Library → Find titles on other sources** turns the automatic search off.
- MangaDex titles now carry all their alternative names, so a title listed under its Korean or romanized name is still found under its English or Russian one.

## Continue

- **Widget "Continue reading"**: covers, titles and chapters of the last three titles; a tap opens the reader where you stopped.
- **Launcher shortcuts**: long-press the Lume icon for "Continue <title>".
- With app lock or "hide in recent apps" on, neither shows your titles.

## Updating

Install it over v0.5.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database moves to version 9 (one new table); nothing existing changes.

## Known limits

- Only titles with the same name (ignoring case, punctuation and "the") are suggested automatically. Spin-offs and side stories with a longer name show up as "Maybe" in Find on other sources and are never linked on their own.
- Linked chapters are matched by chapter number. Chapters without a number on a linked source are left out.
- Downloads moved to another copy stay in their folder on disk.
- The widget opens the chapter you were in; when that chapter is finished, it opens at its start.
