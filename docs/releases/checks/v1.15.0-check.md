# v1.15.0 device check

## Build and device
- Build: debug APK of the full flavour (`app/build/outputs/apk/full/debug/app-full-debug.apk`), package com.lume.app, installed fresh (uninstall, then install with `-r`).
- Emulator: Lume_Light, Android 14 (API 34), 720x1600, wifi disabled (cellular path). ICMP ping to names and IPs got no reply on the emulator, but the catalog and chapters loaded, so network calls worked.
- Mode: Ranobe. Source: NovelFull (Royal Road was not used). Title: "Reincarnation Of The Strongest Sword God", chapter 1 and later chapters.
- No sign-in, no key, no AI request.
- Scope: the novel reader changes since v1.14.0 (`git diff --stat v1.14.0..HEAD`): NovelReaderScreen, NovelBrightness, NovelStatusLine, NovelPagedText, NovelPosition, NovelReaderSettingsSheet, NovelReaderChrome, NovelTextList, NovelReaderViewModel, NovelReaderSettings, AppPreferences, SettingsCatalog.

## Changed areas covered
- Reader settings sheet: "Chapter title in the text", "Brightness on the left edge" (and "Use system brightness" button), "Volume keys turn pages", "Status line", "Paragraph indent" chips, "Auto-scroll speed", "Page by page".
- Reader: chapter title in the text, brightness pill and override, volume keys, status line, indent, auto-scroll play/pause, page-by-page mode, landscape rotation.
- Settings › Reader (Ranobe mode): new controls and settings search for "brightness".
- Crash check over the whole session.
