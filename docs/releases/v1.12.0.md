# Lume v1.12.0

Story notes. While you read, the AI keeps short notes on each chapter: what happened, who was in it, and one fact about each name. The codex fills itself from them, the recap is ready when you come back, and name summaries cost a small request. With AI translation all of this comes in the translation's own answers, without a request of its own.

## New

- **AI story notes** (Settings → Reader, replaces the **AI recap** switch):
  - **Off**: nothing is sent. This is the default.
  - **After each chapter**: a chapter you finish is sent once, in one request that brings its summary, the characters, places, groups and items in it with a fact about each, and how they relate. Opening a title also fills in up to 10 earlier read chapters that have no notes yet, several in one request.
  - **When needed**: nothing is sent while you read. Opening the recap, or summarizing a name, sends the read chapters before your spot that have no notes yet, several in one request.
  - With **AI translation** the notes come in the translation's own answers, so translated chapters need no extra request.

  If you had the AI recap on, story notes are on as **After each chapter**.
- **The codex fills itself.**
  - Names the notes find go straight into the codex, marked **AI**. Their other forms ("Yu San A-san") and their translation become other spellings, so lookup finds them in either language.
  - Forms of one name meet in one entry. A name you already saved only gets its new spellings.
  - Edit an AI entry and it becomes yours. Remove one and it isn't added again.
- **Seen in earlier chapters.** In the lookup sheet (long press on a name), a codex entry lists what each earlier chapter showed about it, the latest last. This costs no request.
- **Summaries from the notes.** **Summarize with AI** writes a short description from those facts, which is a small request. Asking again when there are no newer facts makes no request. A name without facts (read before notes were on) is described as before, from the chapter summaries and the passages that mention it.
- **Summaries on the codex screen.** Each entry shows its summary, if one was written up to where you are reading.

## No spoilers

- An AI entry only shows from the chapter it first appeared in. A chapter translated ahead of you doesn't reveal new names.
- Facts, relations from notes, and summaries written from facts only show from the chapter after the one they come from, because you may still be part way through it.
- Summaries you write at your spot work as before.

## Changes

- **Backups keep the AI's work.** Chapter summaries, facts, relations and codex summaries are now part of backups, because they cost requests to write again. AI entries keep their mark, and removed ones stay removed after a restore.

## Updating

Install it over v1.11.0 as usual. The database moves to version 23; your codex entries stay yours. Backups made with this version use format 9. Older Lume versions restore what they know from them and skip the rest.

## Privacy

Story notes send the text of chapters you have read to the active AI service. With **After each chapter** they are sent as you finish each one; with **When needed**, only when you open the recap or summarize a name. Nothing after your spot is sent, nothing in incognito, and nothing while story notes are off. See [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md).

## For developers

- Database 23 (`MIGRATION_22_23`):
  - `codex_entries.origin` is `user`, `ai` or `ignored`, and `codex_entries.firstChapterId` is new.
  - The new `codex_facts` table holds one fact per `nameKey` and chapter.
  - `codexFor` and `codexOnce` leave `ignored` entries out; `codexWithIgnored` includes them.
- `data/story/StoryNotes.kt`:
  - `ChapterNotes`, `NoteName` and `NoteRelation`, with `parseNotes` and `notesInstructions`.
  - `StoryNotes.save` stores the digest, codex entries (matched by `nameKey`), facts, and relations with a `notes:` origin, once per pair and label.
- `LlmProvider(notes = true)` asks each part for `notes`. `takeNotes()` joins them in chapter order, and `ReaderTranslation.saveNotes` stores them. The digest is stored only when the parts cover most of the chapter.
- `AiRecap.digests` is now the story pass. It takes the reading `order` and uses the `AiFeature.Notes` usage bucket.
- `StoryKnowledge.summarize`:
  - Prefers facts (`facts:` origin, kept up to the last fact's chapter). The fallback is unchanged.
  - Takes `fill`: 4 chapters, or 20 in "When needed" mode.
  - `visibleBefore`, `entryVisible`, `summaryVisible` and `relationVisible` hold the spoiler rules.
- `story_notes_mode` pref (`off`, `finished`, `needed`). `aiRecap` now means "not off". The old `story_ai_recap` switch counts as `finished`.
- Backup format 9 (`docs/BACKUP_FORMAT.md`): codex `origin`, `firstChapterId` and the summary fields, plus `chapterDigests`, `codexFacts` and `codexRelations`.
