# Lume v0.7.8

Logging in to a Lib site with another account works now, going back is instant, and a big library opens at once.

## Fixed

- **Login no longer picks up the old account.** After signing out of MangaLib, HentaiLib or YaoiLib, the login screen could still find the site's old session and close at once with the same account. If Lume is signed out but the site already reports an account before you touch the page, Lume now treats it as the old session: it clears the site's saved data and reloads, and accepts only a login you make yourself. A short note above the site says so.
- **Going back is instant.** The back animation is gone. The back gesture, the back button and the arrow now change the screen at once, the same on every screen. Moving forward and switching tabs look as before.
- **A big library opens at once.** With hundreds of titles, the Bookmarks tab could stay empty for about 10 seconds after starting the app, and it could slow down again whenever chapters or read marks changed. The chapter counts are now worked out in one pass, which is many times faster, and the tab no longer flashes "empty" while it loads.

## Updating

Install it over v0.7.7 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. No database changes.

## Known limits

- When the login screen clears an old site session, it clears all of the app's web data and cookies, except the anti-bot clearance cookies of your sources. Other Lib sites keep their Lume sign-ins but ask for a login the next time their login screen opens.
- If the old account still shows on the site after that, sign out on the site itself, then log in.
- "Sign in with Google" on the site may not work: Google blocks its sign-in inside apps' web views. Use e-mail and password.

## For developers

- `SourceLoginActivity`: when the source has no Lume token, a `localStorage.auth` read before the user's first touch after the first page load is stale. Its access token is never saved; `LibWebSession.wipeAll` (new) deletes all WebView storage and cookies, puts the sources' `cf_clearance` / DDoS-Guard cookies back, and the page reloads once. New `LibAuthStore.parse`.
- `LibWebSession.clear` also expires each cookie with `Secure; SameSite=None`.
- `AppShell`: pop transitions are `EnterTransition.None` / `ExitTransition.None`. `ui/nav/BackGesture.kt`, `BackGestureCard` and the direct `navigationevent-compose` dependency are removed.
- `LibraryDao.observeLibraryStats` uses grouped joins instead of correlated subqueries with `NOT IN`. On a synthetic library of 600 titles and about 300,000 chapters it took 0.4 s instead of 126 s, with identical results. `LibraryViewModel.items` starts as null, so `loaded` waits for the first database answer.
