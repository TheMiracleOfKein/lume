# Lume v0.6.5

A small polish release from a full pass over the app on an emulator.

## Fixes

- **Settings hints.** The hint under the chip rows in **Downloads** (pages downloaded at once), **Network and storage** (DNS over HTTPS) and **Backup and restore** (automatic backups) no longer touches the chips.
- **Reader title bar.** The chapter line under the title is now light on every page, and the dark band behind the bar lasts longer, so it stays readable over light cover pages.
- **Import pack from URL.** Tapping Import with an empty field now says "Enter a pack link first" instead of doing nothing.

## Updating

Install it over v0.6.4 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## For developers

- `SettingsNote` callers pass top/bottom padding; `ReaderTopBar` uses a three-stop gradient; `SourcesViewModel.import()` sends `sources_import_empty` (EN and RU) for an empty field.
