# Lume v0.3.0

Lume now remembers exactly where you stopped, inside the page and for every chapter, and you can pin spots with page bookmarks. The whole app moves more smoothly, and several bugs that hid new chapters or lost downloads are fixed.

## Highlights

- **Exact resume.** Lume remembers the spot inside the page, not just the page number, separately for every chapter. Reopening lands on the same spot, even in very tall webtoon pages, after you rotate the phone, after Android closes the app in the background, and after reading on into the next chapter.
- **Page bookmarks.** In the reader, tap the bookmark button to pin the current spot, then add a note if you like. Hold the button to see all bookmarks of the title. They also appear on the title screen with a picture of the page. Tap one to jump straight back to it; swipe to delete it.
- **Smoother app.** No more jumps when the bottom bar appears or disappears. The pages no longer resize when the reader controls hide. Screens keep their content when you go back, so you no longer see a spinner. Settings opens without switches moving. The back gesture follows your finger, and the side menu follows a swipe.

## Reader

- A chapter counts as **read when you reach its end** (or read on into the next one), not when you open it. Trackers update at the same moment.
- **Add to library** moved to the heart button at the top of the reader. The bookmark button at the bottom now pins the current spot.
- **Previous / next chapter** stays inside the reader, so Back always takes you out of the reader in one step.
- Tapping the title at the top goes back to the title screen instead of opening a second copy of it.
- The controls hide only when you really scroll, not on every touch.
- With double-tap zoom off, a single tap shows or hides the controls immediately.
- Double-tap zoom glides in and out instead of jumping.
- A page that fails to load keeps its place and shows a **Retry** button.
- Reader settings: sliders no longer jump back while you drag them.

## Title and chapters

- **New chapters always appear.** Before, the title screen kept the first chapter list it ever saw. Now it shows the saved list at once and then checks the site. Pull down to check again.
- The chapter list shows how far you got in chapters you started, for example "p. 12 / 40".
- **Continue** opens the exact spot where you stopped.

## Library updates and downloads

- Turning on library updates asks for permission to show notifications (Android 13 and newer).
- Tapping an update notification opens the library.
- Background checks no longer miss sources right after the phone starts.
- Background checks also update the title's chapter list, so new chapters are there when you open it.
- Downloads interrupted because Android closed the app continue on the next start.
- Failed downloads are listed in Downloads with a **Retry** button.

## Other fixes

- **Clear** under "Continue reading" on Home asks before deleting your reading history.
- Titles and chapters whose address contains `+` or `%` open correctly.
- The app is much smaller: about 3 MB instead of 16 MB.

## Backups

- Backups now also contain the position in each chapter, page bookmarks and which chapters are read.
- Older backups still restore.
- Restoring no longer moves your reading position back if the position on the phone is newer.

## Updating

Install it over v0.2.0 as usual. Your library, progress and downloads are kept.

## For developers

- The database is at version 7, with an additive migration from 6. Room schemas are now exported to `app/schemas`.
- New `ReaderViewModel`; the position math is in `ui/screens/ReaderPosition.kt` and covered by unit tests.
- New `data/title/TitleRepository.kt` shows cached title data first, then refreshes it.
- Every source call runs off the main thread (`DispatchingSource` in `SourceRegistry.kt`).
- Release builds use R8. Keep rules are in `app/proguard-rules.pro`. A baseline profile is in `app/src/main/baseline-prof.txt`.
- What comes next is in [docs/ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).
