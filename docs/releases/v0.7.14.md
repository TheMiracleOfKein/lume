# Lume v0.7.14

UI fixes, part one: system bar icons that follow the app theme, borders and accent text you can see, readable selections, a reader whose bars stay readable over light pages, nothing covering lists or buttons, and a confirmation before deleting downloads.

## Fixed

- **Status bar icons match the app theme.** With the app in Light and the phone in dark mode (or the other way round), the clock and icons at the top were the wrong colour and couldn't be seen. They now follow the app's theme. The reader always uses white icons over its dark bars. The site and tracker login screens use your theme too, instead of always dark.
- **Visible borders.** Text fields and outlined buttons (Sources, Trackers, Reset filters, Sign out) had borders that blended into the background. They are now visible in every theme.
- **Readable orange in the Light theme.** Buttons, links and highlighted text use a darker orange on light backgrounds. The bright orange stays for buttons, switches and progress bars.
- **Readable selections.** Selected chapters, selected team chips and selected titles in the Library list were orange on brown and hard to read. Selection is now a soft tint with normal text, and the Library list shows a check mark too.
- **Reader bars.** The page slider and page numbers stay readable over white pages, and the chapter name under the title has a solid backdrop. In landscape, the bars keep clear of the camera cutout and the side navigation bar. A message at the bottom no longer sits on the navigation bar when the bars are hidden.
- **Reader with a light background.** Loading text, error messages, Retry and the chapter divider take their colours from the reader background, so they are readable whatever the app theme is.
- **Nothing covering the content.**
  - On Home without a connection, the list of downloads no longer draws over its caption.
  - A source's catalogue opened from Sources no longer hides its last row under the navigation bar.
  - In landscape, the side navigation shows all its buttons and keeps clear of the cutout.
  - In the compact library grid, the Continue button no longer covers the chapter badges.
  - The library selection bar fits on narrow screens: less common actions are under "More".
- **The Cloudflare check screen** keeps clear of the system bars. Taps no longer go through to the screen behind it, and Back goes back in the page first.
- **Login screens** no longer hide fields behind the keyboard. They show page loading, and Back goes back in the page first.
- **Sheets and dialogs that didn't fit:** the library Display options, the category picker and the WebDAV dialog now scroll.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - Errors in the tracker dialog are shown in red.
  - Read rows in Updates are dimmed less and get a check mark.
  - The library's "hide these" filter is red everywhere.
  - The pull-to-refresh spinner uses Lume's colours.
  - A dark window shows on launch only in the dark theme.

## Safer

- **Deleting downloads asks first.** Deleting a title's downloads or one chapter's download on the Downloads screen now asks for confirmation.
- **Passwords aren't kept in saved screen state.** The WebDAV password, the sync passphrase and tracker passwords are no longer written to Android's saved state when the app is in the background.

## Updating

Install it over v0.7.13 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## For developers

- `LumeColors` gains `outline`, `accentText` and `selection`. `schemeOf` maps every Material 3 role (primary is `accentText`; containers are `selection`), so no baseline colours leak in.
- `ui/theme/SystemBars.kt`: `applyLumeSystemBars(dark)` / `LumeSystemBars(dark)` used by `MainActivity`, `SourceLoginActivity` and `TrackerAuthActivity`. The reader turns off light bar icons while open and restores them.
- `values-night/themes.xml` splits the window theme into light and dark.
- The reader content runs in `LumeTheme(darkTheme = reader background is dark)`.
- `LumeInsets.sides` and `listBottomPadding()` in `ShellInsets.kt`. The nav rail scrolls and shrinks its slots under 480 dp of height.
- `components/RefreshIndicator.kt`: `LumeRefreshIndicator`.
