## Problems found

### Serious

None found.

### Minor

1. **"Second Life Ranker" opens a title with a slug name and no chapters.** Steps: open Solo Leveling, scroll to "Similar titles", tap "Second Life Ranker". The app opens a title named "second-life-ranker-7ba78ec7", "Chapter list (0)", Read disabled. The single-result rule accepted the match, so the cause is probably the source's record for this title, not the feature. Not checked on the source site. Worth a look before release.
2. **Back from a catalogue opened by a similar-title lookup clears the search first.** Steps: tap "Omniscient Reader" on the Solo Leveling screen, press Back. The catalogue stays and its search field is cleared (Popular list). A second Back returns to the title. This is probably the catalogue's normal search behaviour, not checked against v1.1.0. **Fixed after the check:** a catalogue opened straight on a search now goes back with one Back.
3. **Spinner not confirmed.** The spinner over the tapped cover was not seen in any screenshot. Lookups on Asura Scans finished quickly, so this may be a timing issue rather than a missing spinner.
4. **Reader highlight not confirmed.** Settings search "text size" in Ranobe opens the Reader page, but the highlight had faded (or not appeared) by the time of the screenshot.
5. **Menu lists "Downloads" in Ranobe mode.** The Settings page hides Downloads in Ranobe mode, but the Menu entry stays. Not in the change list; the user decides whether the Menu entry should hide too.
6. **Import feedback not seen.** After "Import books…" the book appeared in Local books, but no result text was visible on the Library page after scrolling to its end (the same as v1.1.0 Minor 7).

## Notes
- Settings search "volume" in both modes returns "Translate chapter labels" (its keywords match "Том" = volume). It appears in Manga and Ranobe alike, so it is not a mode problem.
- The Asura Scans search returned no results for most of the recommended titles tested. The not-found dialog is the designed outcome for those, but the source's own search was not checked to confirm.
- Mode switching was done from Menu > Mode (the logo long press was not tried).
