# Lume v0.9.7

Bring your library over from Kotatsu or your tracker's reading list, and keep downloads in any folder you like.

## New

- **Import Kotatsu backups.** Settings → Backup → restore from a file now also takes a Kotatsu backup (`.bk.zip`). Favourites from sources Lume has join your library with their categories. Titles already in the library keep their place and get the categories added. Titles from sources Lume doesn't have are listed as skipped. Reading history isn't imported: Kotatsu doesn't store chapter numbers, so Lume can't tell which chapters you've read.
- **Import a tracker reading list.** Signed in to AniList or MyAnimeList? Settings → Trackers → **Import reading list** turns your "Reading" list into library entries. Each title is searched on your sources with the same matching as "Found for you", and only clear matches are added. Added titles are linked to the tracker, and the chapters you've already read are marked read, with "Continue" on the last one. A card shows the progress (you can leave the screen or cancel) and then lists the titles no source had. Long lists take a while, because every title is searched on every enabled source.
- **Any folder as download storage.** Settings → Downloads → Storage → **Choose a folder…** saves new downloads to any folder you pick, for example on an SD card or in Documents. Chapters go into a `Lume` folder inside it. If Lume loses access to the folder, settings show **Reconnect** and new downloads go to internal storage until you reconnect. Downloads you already have stay where they are and keep working.

## Updating

Install it over v0.9.6 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. There are no database changes.

## Privacy

Importing a reading list sends your tracker sign-in to AniList or MyAnimeList to read the list, and the titles' names and authors to your sources to find them. Nothing happens until you tap **Import reading list**. [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md) lists it.

## For developers

- Kotatsu: `data/backup/KotatsuBackup.kt` (`KotatsuBackupFormat.parse`, `matchSource`, `linkCandidates`) reads only the `categories` and `favourites` entries, each capped and with one total budget. `BackupRepository.restoreFromUri` sends zip files to `importKotatsu` and returns `RestoreResult.Kotatsu`. The category lookup by name (`categoryIdByName`) is shared with the Mihon import.
- Tracker lists: `Tracker.supportsListImport` and `fetchReadingList()`, implemented by `AniListTracker` (`Viewer`, then `MediaListCollection(status: CURRENT)`) and `MyAnimeListTracker` (`users/@me/mangalist?status=reading` with paging). `TrackerListImport` runs on the app scope with two entries at a time and searches through `TitleSourceRepository.find("", details)`. `chooseListCandidate` keeps `TitleMatch.Same` and prefers the exact name. Titles are added with `discover = false`.
- Folder storage: `downloads_root` holds a file path or a tree URI. `SafStorage` wraps `DocumentsContract` and caches document ids. `DownloadLocations` holds the layout helpers. Chapters are built in `downloads_staging` and then copied into `<tree>/Lume/...` as `.lume-part` and renamed. `pageData` passes `content://` pages to Coil. The webtoon region decoder opens them through a file descriptor, falling back to a stream. CBZ files in a folder are copied to `cache/cbz_src` before they're unpacked.
- Device check: [v0.9.7-check.md](v0.9.7-check.md).
