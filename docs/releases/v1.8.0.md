# Lume v1.8.0

Story lookup: long-press a name to see who it is, and a "Previously…" recap when you come back to a long series.

## New

- **Name lookup.** Long-press a name or term in the text reader (scroll and page mode) to open a sheet with:
  - where it **first appeared**, and in **how many chapters** it is mentioned;
  - its **latest mentions**; tap one to see the paragraphs around it (your reading spot stays where it is);
  - its glossary translation, if it has one.
  Names of several words ("Lin Feng") are picked up whole, and the search can be edited in the sheet. In a translated chapter the sheet also has **Show original**.
- **No spoilers.** Lookup only searches chapters up to the paragraph you pressed, even when later chapters are downloaded.
- **Works offline.** It searches the text of chapters you have read or downloaded, kept in an index on the phone. The sheet says how many chapters up to here were searched. Nothing is indexed in incognito.
- **Codex.** Save a name from the sheet with its kind (character, place, group, item, term), other spellings and your own note. Every title has its own codex screen (the book icon in the reader's top bar): search, sort, add, edit and delete. Saved spellings are searched together.
- **"Previously…"** When you come back to a title after a break, a sheet shows the last paragraphs before your saved spot, from the end of the previous chapter when needed. Pick the break in Settings → Reader: 6 hours, 1 day (default), 3 days, or off.
- **Settings → Reader** has a switch for the story index, the recap choice, and the index size with **Clear story index**.

## Updating

Install it over v1.7.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads, glossaries, sign-ins and tracker links are kept. The database gets new tables for the chapter text index and the codex (an additive change, tested). Chapters read before this version are indexed the next time you open them; downloaded chapters are indexed when a lookup needs them. Backups now use format version 7 with the codex; older backups still load. The text index is not part of backups, since it can be rebuilt.

## Privacy

Nothing leaves the phone. The index and the codex are stored on the phone only; the codex is part of your backups.

## For developers

- Room 18 → 19: `chapter_text` (plain text per block, original language) with an FTS4 content table `chapter_text_fts` (unicode61), and `codex_entries` / `codex_aliases`. The codex has reserved `summary`, `summaryOrigin` and `summaryUpToChapterId` columns for later generated descriptions, kept spoiler-safe by the chapter they cover.
- `StoryIndex` fills the index from `NovelBook.loadChapter` (off the reading path) and backfills downloaded chapters on demand. `StoryLookup` returns a `StoryContext` (term, codex entry, glossary target, first and recent mentions, coverage): the input a later story provider can use next to the `TranslationProvider` seam.
- Backup format 7 (`codexEntries` with nested `aliases`). The codex is not in sync yet.
