# Lume v0.6.1

A small update. The library shows how many sources each title comes from, the app icon and notification icon are fixed, YaoiLib pages load again, and Android's backup no longer uploads your sign-ins or stops because of large downloads.

## Highlights

- **Sources badge in the library.** Titles with linked sources show a small blue badge with the number of sources, next to the unread and downloaded counts. Turn it off in **Library → Display**.
- **A proper app icon.** The launcher icon is sharp and follows your launcher's shape, and on Android 13 and newer it can follow your themed icons.
- **Notification icon.** Download and library-update notifications show the Lume mark in the status bar instead of a white square.
- **YaoiLib works again.** Its main image server stopped serving pages; Lume now tries the site's other image servers and remembers the one that works. This applies to MangaLib and HentaiLib too.

## Fixes

- **HentaiLib** shows chapters only to signed-in users. Instead of "HTTP 404", the reader now says to sign in under **Sources → HentaiLib**.
- **Android backup.**
  - Cloud backup no longer uploads tracker sign-ins, site sign-ins or cookies.
  - Downloads, imported local files and Lume's own backups stay out too. Before, a large download folder could stop Android's backup completely.
  - Your library is still backed up. Moving to a new phone with a cable or Wi-Fi transfer still copies everything.
  - Settings now travel only with Lume's own backups (**Settings → Backup**).
- **Russian texts.** "N titles with new chapters" and "Download next N" use the right word forms for every number. The Mihon and local import messages read correctly for any count.
- **Launcher shortcuts.** Using a "Continue" shortcut tells the launcher, so it ranks your shortcuts better.
- The side menu follows a swipe more smoothly, and the navigation rail decision uses the real window size (split screen, freeform windows).

## Updating

Install it over v0.6.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## Known limits

- MangaRead and ToonGod often show Cloudflare checks; Lume opens them in its built-in browser.
- HentaiLib needs a signed-in account to read.

## For developers

- New `engineConfig` key `pagesNeedLogin` (LibSocial) and `SourceLoginRequiredException`. LibSocial picks the first image server that serves a chapter's first page (`orderedImageServers`, unit-tested).
- `ReaderScreen.kt` is split into `ReaderScreen`, `ReaderChrome`, `ReaderPage` and `ReaderSettingsSheet`. AniList, Shikimori and `TrackerSync` each have their own file. No behaviour change.
- Lint reports no issues (36 warnings before). AGP 9.4.1, Kotlin 2.4.21, Gradle 9.8.1, Robolectric 4.17; the unused OkHttp logging interceptor is gone.
- CI also builds the R8 release APK. `live-sources.yml` checks every bundled source and catalogue pack against the live sites weekly. Dependabot covers Gradle and GitHub Actions.
