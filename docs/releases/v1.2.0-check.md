## Problems

1. **MangaLib title page returns HTTP 404 (needs confirmation: possible regression in the shared Lib code).** Severity: serious if it is a regression; otherwise minor (site data).
   - Steps to reproduce: Manga mode, Source = MangaLib (Menu > Source). Catalog > Popular. Tap "Тайный союз". The title page shows "Not found on api.cdnlibs.org (HTTP 404). The title or chapter may have been removed." with a Retry button. Retry shows the same message (twice).
   - Not yet checked: whether v1.1.2 opens the same title, and whether other MangaLib titles load (only "Дни менеджеров" and "Волк и найдёныш" were opened, and both were YaoiLib-sourced at the time). Compare with v1.1.2 before release.
   - **Resolved after the walk: not a regression, and not MangaLib.** The list was YaoiLib's: "Тайный союз" (`23435--secret-alliance`), "Выжить в романе" and "Волк и найдёныш" are YaoiLib titles (site 2). Asked directly, the API answers YaoiLib title details with 404 `{"toast":{"message":"Not Found"}}` without a sign-in, while its catalogue and chapter list answer 200. v1.1.2 behaves the same (the YaoiLib live check fails identically on it). MangaLib title details, chapters and pages pass the live check on this build. Noted in the roadmap's known problems.
2. **Sort control in the chapter list is hard to use.** Severity: minor. The Sort icon (top right of the chapter list) sometimes did not apply on the first tap and sometimes opened a chapter row; the list screen lags behind the taps. Not confirmed as an app bug (could be the emulator's rendering lag); re-check on a physical device.
3. **Filter sheet Apply needed a second tap.** Severity: minor. The first tap on Apply (at the bottom of the sheet) did not close the sheet; the second did.
