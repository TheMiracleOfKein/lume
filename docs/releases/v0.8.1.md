# Lume v0.8.1

A fix for zoom in the webtoon reader: a zoomed-in page no longer twitches when you move it.

## Fixed

- **Webtoon zoom.** After zooming in and moving the page, the picture twitched a moment after you let go. The sharp version of the visible part was dropped while the next one was being decoded, so the blurry page flashed in between, and the sharp part was placed rounded to whole pixels, so it jumped slightly against the page under it. Now the sharp part stays on screen until the next one is ready, a small move inside the part already decoded doesn't decode anything again, and the sharp part is drawn exactly over the pixels it came from.

## Updating

Install it over v0.8.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## For developers

- `PageDetail` keeps the last `DecodedDetail` until a new one is decoded and draws it with a float transform over the source rectangle actually decoded (`decodeDetail`).
- `WebtoonReader` keeps a page's detail region while the zoom is unchanged and the on-screen region stays inside it. New `PageRect.contains(PageRect)`, covered in `PageAnalysisTest`.
- Released without the emulator check and smoke test, at the owner's request.
