# Lume v0.7.16

Fixes for Home and two Russian sources: the list on Home no longer slides when you come back to it, ReManga works again, and MangaBuff shows only real titles, with covers.

## Fixed

- **Home.** With titles in "Continue reading", coming back to Home from another screen made the titles under "Now reading" slide in from above. The list now stays where it was.
- **ReManga.** The site moved its catalogue, search, title, chapter list and pages to a new API, so everything answered "404". Lume now uses the new API. Long chapter lists load in full. "Popular" is sorted by votes, because the site's plain rating now puts unrated titles first. The sort list starts with "By votes".
- **MangaBuff.** Home and the catalogue showed site collections, for example "за 2026 год", as if they were titles. They had no cover and opened to a 404. Lume now reads only the title cards, and takes their covers from where the site actually keeps them.

## Updating

Install it over v0.7.15 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change. ReManga titles in your library keep working: their ids didn't change.

## For developers

- `HomeViewModel.history` is a `StateFlow` started eagerly (20 most recent entries). Static items on Home have stable keys.
- `RemangaEngine` uses `/api/v2/` for catalogue, search, title, chapters and pages; the filter form stays on `/api/forms/titles/`. Paging follows `next`, `meta.total_pages` or `props.total_pages`.
- `MangaBuffEngine.parseCatalog` reads `.cards__item` cards only (links with a poster image as a fallback) and takes covers from `data-src`, `srcset` or a CSS background.
- New JVM test: `RuSourceParsingTest`. Source packs: ReManga 1.2.0, MangaBuff 1.1.0.
