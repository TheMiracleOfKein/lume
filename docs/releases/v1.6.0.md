# Lume v1.6.0

Read aloud: listen to novels like an audiobook.

## New

- **Read aloud.** The headphones button on the text reader's bottom bar reads the chapter with Android's own text-to-speech, starting at the text on screen, and goes on to the next chapter by itself.
  - It keeps playing with the screen off and after you leave the reader. Play/pause and back / forward one paragraph are in the notification, on the lock screen and on headphone buttons. Unplugging headphones or a phone call pauses it.
  - The sentence being read is highlighted, and the text scrolls (or turns pages) along with it. Scroll away by hand and the text stays put; **Back to the voice** returns to it.
  - **Speed**, **Voice** and, with more than one installed, **Engine**. By default a Russian voice reads Russian text and an English one English text, when the engine has them. Voices that need internet are marked; the rest work offline.
  - **Sleep timer:** at the end of the chapter, or after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes.
  - The spot is saved like normal reading progress, so **Continue reading** opens the book where the voice stopped, and chapters read to their end are marked read. Incognito keeps nothing.
  - No Google services needed: any installed engine works (Google, Samsung, RHVoice, eSpeak…). Without one, Lume says so and opens Android's text-to-speech settings.

## Updating

Install it over v1.5.6 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads, sign-ins and tracker links are kept. No database change. Read aloud settings stay on the phone and are not part of backups or sync, since voices differ between phones.

## For developers

- `:feature:novel` now depends on Media3 (`media3-session`, `media3-common`): `ReadAloudService` is a `MediaSessionService` around a `SimpleBasePlayer` (`TtsPlayer`) that follows the process-wide `ReadAloud` controller. The manga build doesn't include it.
- `ReadAloudEngine` is plain logic over a `Speaker` interface (sentence queue with a generation number per seek or pause, chapter hand-over, sleep timer, progress saves) and has JVM tests with a fake speaker. `SpeechScript` splits blocks into sentences with offsets into the drawn text, for the highlight.
- Chapter loading and progress writes moved from `NovelReaderViewModel` into `NovelBook`, shared by the reader and read aloud.
- Android gives media keys to the session of the app that plays audio, and the speech is played by the TTS engine's process. So while speaking, `ReadAloud` also plays a silent `AudioTrack`; that makes Lume's session the media button session, and headset and keyboard keys reach it.
- New permissions in the novel module: `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` and `WAKE_LOCK` (held only while speaking), and a `<queries>` entry for TTS engines.
