# Lume v0.7.11

Reader and navigation: very tall webtoon pages draw again, the reader always has a way back, and links no longer throw away the screens you had open.

## Fixed

- **Very tall webtoon pages draw instead of staying black.** Some sources (Asura Scans among them) send each page as one strip about 15,000 pixels tall, more than the phone's graphics can draw at once, so the page stayed black. Lume now draws such a page as a stack of slices, at full sharpness.
- **Links and notifications keep your place.** Opening a title or a chapter from a notification, a shared link or a widget now opens it on top of what you had open, and Back returns there. Before, it closed every screen and started over from Home.
- **The menu doesn't open the same screen twice.** Picking a screen that is already open no longer stacks a second copy that Back had to close.
- **The reader always has a way back.** While a chapter loads, or when it fails to load, the reader shows a top bar with Back and the title, next to Retry.
- **The first and last chapter are clear.** The previous and next chapter buttons are greyed out at the first and last chapter, and pulling past the end shows "This is the last chapter" (or the first) instead of doing nothing. Next and previous follow reading order, whatever order the source lists the chapters in.
- **Pages show a spinner while they load,** and the empty space they take up is closer to a real page, so scrolling jumps less.
- **Screen readers announce the back arrow** as "Back" on every screen.

## Updating

Install it over v0.7.10 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. No database changes.

## For developers

- `AppShell.openLink`: tab routes still use `navigateToTab`; `read/…` routes replace an open reader; other routes are pushed with `launchSingleTop`. Menu entries use `launchSingleTop`.
- `ui/components/BackButton` with the new `cd_back` string, used by every top bar.
- `ReaderBackBar` over the reader's loading and error states.
- `domain/ChapterNeighbours.chapterNeighbours` picks the previous and next chapter in reading order; the reader disables the buttons at the ends and shows an edge notice (drag past the end, tap zone, volume key, paged turn).
- `ReaderPage`: one `zoomLayer` modifier and one `detectPinchAndPan` gesture for both zoom frames; per-page spinner; unknown-aspect placeholder at 70% of the window height.
- `ReaderTallPage` and `TallPageTiles`: in webtoon mode, a page still taller than 4096 px after Coil's capped decode is redrawn from the original file with `BitmapRegionDecoder`, in slices of at most 2048 decoded px, with a power-of-two sample size that keeps the width at or above the screen width.
- New tests: `ChapterNeighboursTest`, `TallPageTilesTest`.
