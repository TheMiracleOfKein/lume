# Lume v1.9.0

AI with your own key: chapter translation that keeps names the same in every chapter, a "story so far" when you come back, and codex notes on characters and their relations, all without spoilers.

## New

- **AI translation.** The text reader's translation sheet has a fourth engine, **AI (your key)**. It uses a language model with your own API key:
  - **OpenRouter** (one key for many models; the default model picks one for you), **OpenAI**, **DeepSeek**, **Gemini**, or **Other (URL)** for any OpenAI-compatible server, such as Ollama or LM Studio on your computer (no key needed);
  - **Choose…** lists the service's models, with prices on OpenRouter;
  - it handles names, tone and dialogue better than the other engines, and costs what the service charges.
- **Names stay the same.** Every request carries the title's glossary and the last translated paragraphs. The model also lists the names it meets for the first time (characters, places, organizations, items, techniques). They go into the glossary as **Suggested** and are used straight away, so the next paragraphs and chapters write them the same way.
  - In the glossary, **Accept**, edit or **Reject** each one, or **Accept all**. A rejected name isn't suggested again.
  - An accepted character, place, group or item is also added to the codex, with its translation as another spelling, so name lookup finds it in either language.
- **Style note.** A short note per title for AI translation, for example "keep -san and -sama". **Translate this chapter again** asks the model again for the chapter you're reading.
- **AI recap.** Settings → Reader → **AI recap** (off by default). Each chapter you finish gets a short summary, kept on the phone. "Previously…" then adds **The story so far**: the last four chapters and the current one up to your spot, in the language you read the title in. This is also the recap in the translation.
- **Codex summaries and relations.** In the name lookup, a saved name has **Summarize with AI**. It describes the character, place or organization from what you have read up to that spot, and lists its relations to other names ("sister", "mentor", "member of"). Relations show on both names and can be removed.
- **No spoilers.** The recap and summaries use only chapters up to your spot. A summary or relation written further on in the story stays hidden from a reader further back; **Update summary up to here** writes it again for where you are.

## Fixes

- The app no longer closes when you tap **Continue** after passing a Cloudflare check (seen on Royal Road after clearing cookies).

## Updating

Install it over v1.8.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads, glossaries, codex, sign-ins and tracker links are kept. The database gets three additive changes (glossary term status and kind, a style note per title, and new tables for chapter summaries and codex relations), each tested. Existing glossary terms count as your own. Backups now use format version 8, which adds the term status and kind and the style note; older backups still load. Chapter summaries and relations are not part of backups, since they can be made again.

## Privacy

Nothing changes unless you pick **AI (your key)**. With it, the chapter text you read in that title, its glossary and style note go to the service you chose, with your key. **AI recap** and **Summarize with AI** send chapter text, chapter summaries and the passages that mention a name, only up to your spot. Nothing is sent in incognito. Keys are stored encrypted on the phone and are never part of backups or sync. See [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md).

## For developers

- `feature/novel/llm/`: `LlmClient` for any OpenAI-compatible `/chat/completions` and `/models`, with `LlmPreset` (OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Gemini, custom URL). Keys are kept per service in the encrypted private store (`translate_llm_<service>_secret`). It asks for `response_format: json_object` and asks again without it when a server rejects that. Answers are read with tolerant JSON extraction.
- `LlmProvider` is a `TranslationProvider` that returns paragraphs by id plus new terms. Those go through `filterSuggestions` and are stored as `suggested`. Providers now set their own run sizes, and `glossaryInCacheKey = false` keeps cached AI chapters when the glossary grows; cached HTML keeps marks by source (`data-gs`).
- Room 19 → 20 (`glossary_terms.status`, `glossary_terms.kind`, `title_prefs.translateStyle`), 20 → 21 (`chapter_digests`), 21 → 22 (`codex_relations`, by name with `upToChapterId`). The codex's reserved `summary`, `summaryOrigin` and `summaryUpToChapterId` columns are now filled; `visibleAt` keeps them spoiler-safe.
- Backup format 8. Sync carries the glossary status and kind and the style note.
