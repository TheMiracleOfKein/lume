# Lume v0.7.6

The MangaLib import stops tripping the site's request limit and brings only your reading lists. Update checks look at up to 20 titles you choose with the bell. Search shows which titles are already in your library.

## New

- **Request limit for the Lib sites.** MangaLib, HentaiLib and YaoiLib share one API that refuses bursts. Lume now sends it at most one request every half second, so long imports no longer stop halfway. A failed chapter list is retried after 5 seconds.
- **Only reading lists are imported.** Import bookmarks brings over "Reading", "Planned", "Dropped" and "Read". Favourites and your own folders stay on the site.
- **"Read" means read.** Titles in the site's "Read" list have every chapter marked read.
- **The bell chooses what updates check.** Update now and the scheduled check look only at titles with the bell on their page, up to 20. With hundreds of titles in the library, a check used to take very long. Until you ring any bell, the check looks at the 20 titles you read last. The Updates screen says which applies. The bell now shows for every library title; in "Subscribed" download mode, bell titles still download their new chapters.
- **Read of total in the library.** The library badge shows chapters read of all chapters ("12/150") instead of the unread count. It stays accent-coloured while unread chapters remain and turns grey when you're caught up.
- **Library status in search.** Search results already in your library get a colour bar: green for Reading, blue for Planned, red for Dropped, purple for Read, and the accent colour for any other category. The status comes from the title's categories (an import creates them).

## Updating

Install it over v0.7.5 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. No database changes.

Titles you had subscribed to with the bell keep it and are now the ones update checks look at.

## Known limits

- The search bar only marks results from the same source as the library title; a title saved from another source isn't matched.
- The status colours know the standard list names in Russian and English ("Читаю", "Reading", "Брошено", "Dropped" and so on).
- An import that's already in the library isn't re-filtered: titles imported earlier from favourites or custom folders stay until you remove them.

## For developers

- `SourceRegistry` gives every `libsocial` source a host rate limit of `LIB_API_RATE_LIMIT` (2 req/s) on its API host unless the manifest sets `engineConfig.rateLimit`.
- `planLibBookmarkImport` keeps `LIB_IMPORTED_STATUSES` (1–4) only; `LibImportTitle.completed` marks the "read" status, and `libAllChaptersRead` builds its target. JVM tests in `LibBookmarksTest`.
- `title_prefs.autoDownload` is now the bell (same column, no migration). `LibraryDao.watchedBookmarks`, `observeWatchedCount`, `recentBookmarks`; `WATCHED_TITLES_LIMIT = 20`. `LibraryUpdateWorker` checks watched titles or, with none, the 20 most recently read.
- New `domain/LibraryStatus` (`libraryStatusOf`, tests in `LibraryStatusTest`) and `ui/components/LibraryMarks`. `TitleListRow` and `CoverCard` take an optional `marker` colour.
