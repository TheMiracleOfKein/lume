# Lume v1.5.5

Guided panel view you can rely on.

## Fixed

- **Guided panel view no longer stops responding.** If you tapped or swiped while the reader was still zooming to a panel or turning a page, guided view could stop for the rest of the session: taps neither moved to the next panel nor turned the page. A cut-short animation now ends only that one step. Pages you swipe away from start whole again when you come back, and the reader no longer skips panels on a page that hasn't finished loading.

## New

- **Panel order.** Guided view now reads the panels in a row right to left by default, as manga are drawn. Choose "Left to right (comics)" for western comics. This is separate from the page-turn direction, so you can turn pages either way and keep manga panel order. It's in Settings → Reader and, while guided view is on, in the reader's settings.
- **The rest of the page is dimmed.** While a panel is in focus, everything around it darkens, so it's clear what to look at. The dim follows the zoom when you pinch or pan.
- **Panels include what breaks out of their frames.** Speech bubbles and drawings that spill over a panel's border into the gap or the page margin now belong to that panel: the zoom and the bright area include them. A bubble that reaches into the next panel no longer joins the two panels.

## Updating

Install it over v1.5.4 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads, sign-ins and tracker links are kept. No database change.

## For developers

- `PageAnalysis.panels` grows each panel found by the XY cut with the ink joined to its frame from outside (`InkMap.withOverflow`): an 8-connected flood from the frame's edge ring, within `AnalysisTuning.overflowMargin` of the panel and outside the other panels. Bands (webtoon strips) are unchanged.
- `ReaderSettings.panelOrder` (`PANELS_RTL` / `PANELS_LTR`, DataStore key `reader_panel_order`) picks the panel order; the reader mode no longer does.
- `PagedReader` runs each page turn in `interruptible { }`, which absorbs the `CancellationException` of an interrupted zoom or pager animation instead of ending the turn collector. Guided steps and zoom states are keyed by strip item key, not index. `PanelSpotlight` draws the dim from the zoom's live content bounds.
