# Lume v0.8.0

Reader 2.0: sharp zoom on every page, layouts that fit the screen, and a guided view that walks through a page panel by panel. All of it runs on the phone, with no model and no account.

## New

- **Sharp zoom.** Zooming in no longer just enlarges the picture. In the paged modes the page is decoded again at the zoom you chose, piece by piece, so small text stays crisp up to 5×. In webtoon mode the part of the strip on screen is decoded again once you stop moving, and dropped when you zoom out.
- **Guided panel view.** In the paged modes, turn on "Guided panel view" in the reader settings, or with the new button in the reader's top bar. Each page starts whole; then each tap or page turn zooms to the next panel in reading order (right to left in right-to-left mode), and after the last panel comes the next page. Lume finds the panels itself, from the gutters between them. Pages where it finds only one panel turn as usual.
- **Crop borders.** Plain white or black margins are cut off, so the drawing fills the screen.
- **Split double pages.** In portrait, a page wider than tall is shown as two halves, one after the other, in reading order.
- **Two pages side by side.** In landscape (or always, if you want), pages are shown in pairs, like a book. "First page alone" keeps the cover by itself so the pairs after it match the printed book. Double pages always stand alone.
- **The previous chapter above.** In webtoon mode with seamless reading, scrolling up past the first page shows the end of the previous chapter, without a jump.
- **Pages loaded ahead.** Choose how many pages Lume loads before you reach them (0 to 10, 4 by default).
- **See exactly where you stopped.** "Continue reading" on Home shows the panel you stopped at instead of the cover, and page bookmarks on the title screen show the panel (or the part of a webtoon strip) you bookmarked, instead of a slice of the page. Titles you haven't opened since the update keep their cover until you read them again.
- **Two panes on tablets.** On screens 840 dp wide or more, the title screen shows the details on the left and the chapter list on the right.

The new options are under Settings → Reader → Pages and in the reader's own settings, and settings search finds them.

## Changed

- Images now load through Coil 3, the current version of the image library. Pages cached by older versions are cleared once and loaded again.

## Updating

Install it over v0.7.16 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database gets one new column (version 11 → 12) on the first start. The image cache starts empty, so pages you read before load from the network once more. Guided panel view, cropping and splitting start off; two pages side by side start on "Landscape".

## For developers

- Coil 3.6 (`coil3`, `coil-network-okhttp`) with the same OkHttp headers; the disk cache moved to `image_cache_v3`. Telephoto 0.19 (`zoomable-image-coil3`) for the paged modes.
- `domain/page/PageAnalysis`: `LumaGrid`, `contentBounds` and a recursive XY cut for `panels` in `PanelOrder.LeftToRight`, `RightToLeft` or `Vertical`; `spotPanel` for bookmark thumbnails. `domain/page/PageFit.placeRegion` places a page so a region fits (or fills) a viewport.
- `data/page/PageAnalyzer` (in `AppContainer`) analyses a 400 px copy through Coil and keeps 300 results in memory. Nothing is stored or synced.
- `ReaderStripItem.Page.part` (`PagePart`) and `ReaderStripItem.Spread`; `buildReaderStrip(loaded, StripLayout)` lays out split pages and spreads; `stripIndexOf` / `anchorAt` map a split page's second half to offset 0.5 and a spread to its first page.
- `ReaderPaged.kt` (`PagedReader`, guided panel steps) and `ReaderPagedPage.kt` (`PagedPageImage`, `SpreadPages`); webtoon detail through `zoomedVisibleRegion` and `PageDetail`.
- `ReaderViewModel`: `warmAhead` (preload with sizes), `prefetchPreviousChapter`, `onWideScreen`, strip rebuilds when a page turns out to be a double page.
- Database 11 → 12: `reading_progress.pageImageUrl` (nullable, written by the reader, left out of backups and sync); `PageSpotImage` shows its spot panel on Home's Continue cards and in bookmark thumbnails.
- New `ReaderSettings` fields: `preloadPages`, `cropBorders`, `splitWide`, `spreads`, `spreadCoverAlone`, `guidedPanels`.
- `ScrollBenchmark` (`FrameTimingMetric`) for the catalogue grid and the webtoon reader, next to `StartupBenchmark`.
- New tests: `PageAnalysisTest`, `ReaderStripLayoutTest`, more in `TallPageTilesTest`, and `migrate11To12KeepsProgressAndAddsPageImage` in `MigrationTest`.
