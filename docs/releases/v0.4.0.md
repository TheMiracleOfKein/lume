# Lume v0.4.0

The library gets covers, sorting, filters, search and counts on every title. A new Updates screen collects new chapters from your whole library. The chapter list can mark chapters read, filter them and download several at once. On MangaLib, YaoiLib and HentaiLib, each chapter now appears once instead of once per translation team.

## Highlights

- **A new library.** Choose between a cover grid (the new default), a compact grid with the title on the cover, and the old list. Sort by title, last read, date added, unread chapters or latest chapter. Filter by unread, downloaded, started, tracking and completed: tap a filter once to keep only matching titles, twice to hide them. Search the library by title or alternative title.
- **Updates.** The bottom bar now has **Updates** where History used to be. It lists new chapters from your whole library by the day they were found. Tap one to read it, tap the arrow to download it, hold it to mark it read. Pull down or tap **Update now** to check the library right away. History is in the side menu.
- **One copy per chapter.** On MangaLib, YaoiLib and HentaiLib, a chapter translated by several teams used to appear once per team. Now it appears once, from the team with the most chapters. Team chips above the list let you pick a team or show every copy.

## Library

- Each title shows how many chapters are **unread** (orange) and **downloaded** (grey). Both can be turned off in the library sheet.
- **Select several titles:** hold a title to start, tap to add more, hold another title to select everything in between. Then set categories, mark all chapters read or unread, download the unread chapters, or remove the titles from the library.
- **Categories** has its own screen (the pencil next to the category chips): add, rename, delete and reorder categories. Deleting a category keeps its titles in the library.
- **Default category:** titles you add to the library go straight into it.
- Library settings are remembered.

## Updates

- New chapters are found by comparing chapter ids, not by counting chapters. A site that removes one chapter and adds another still reports the new one.
- A chapter that another team already translated is not reported again.
- Update notifications open the Updates screen.
- A progress bar shows a running check, and "Checked 5 minutes ago" shows the last one.
- After this update, Lume first learns the chapters of every library title, so the first check does not report old chapters as new.

## Chapters

- **Hold a chapter** to select it; hold another to select everything in between. Then mark read, mark unread, download or delete the downloads. With one chapter selected, **mark previous as read** marks every chapter before it.
- Reading or marking one team's copy of a chapter marks the other copies too.
- **Filters:** unread, read, downloaded, with page bookmarks. Sort oldest or newest first. Both are saved for each title.
- **Download menu:** next 1, 5 or 10 unread chapters, all unread chapters, or delete every downloaded chapter of the title.
- The chapter list now starts with the newest chapter. The sort button switches to oldest first, and the choice is saved for the title.
- The reader's next and previous chapter follow the same list, continuing with the team of the chapter you opened.
- Marking chapters read also updates your trackers, never moving them backwards.

## Faster start

- The app ships a **measured startup profile**, recorded on a real emulator through start-up, the tabs, a title, its chapters and the reader. It replaces the hand-written one from v0.3.

## Updating

Install it over v0.3.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept.

## For developers

- The database is at version 8, with an additive migration from 7: a `library_chapters` table and chapter-list settings in `title_prefs`. The migration is tested (`app/src/sharedTest`), on Robolectric in CI and on a device with `connectedDebugAndroidTest`. Room's test helper doesn't run under Robolectric on Windows, so there it is skipped.
- `AppContainer` holds the shared objects. Screens get it through `LocalAppContainer`, ViewModels through their constructor. `LumeApp.instance` remains only for `appString()`.
- Every screen now has a ViewModel. New ones: Library, Updates, History, Downloads, Settings, Sources and Categories.
- New pure logic with unit tests: `domain/LibraryQuery.kt` (library filters and sorting), `domain/ChapterDedupe.kt` (one copy per chapter), `domain/ChapterList.kt` (chapter filters and "previous" chapters), `data/library/LibraryChapters.kt` (new chapters by id). Reading order and tracker matching are tested as well.
- `LibraryRepository` holds the shared writes: adding to and removing from the library, categories, read marks.
- New `:baselineprofile` module. `gradlew :app:generateBaselineProfile` records the profile on an attached device (API 33+). `StartupBenchmark` measures cold start with and without it.
- GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) builds, runs the unit tests and lint on every push and pull request.
- What comes next is in [docs/ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).
