# Lume v0.6.2

Settings are easier to find your way around. They are split into sections, settings that need it explain what they do, and a search field finds any setting by name or by a related word. Android's cloud backup keeps your settings again, without your sign-ins.

## Highlights

- **Settings in sections.** The settings page now lists ten sections: Appearance, Reader, Library and updates, Downloads, Sources and search, Trackers, Backup and restore, Privacy, Network and storage, and About. Each one shows what it holds and opens its own page.
- **Search in settings.** Type in the field at the top of **Settings**. Lume looks through the names of settings, their sections, their explanations and related words, so "oled", "mobile data" or "бэкап" find the right setting too. Tap a result to open its section; the setting is scrolled into view and briefly highlighted.
- **Explanations.** Settings whose effect isn't clear from the name have a short line under them. Examples: what the reader's tap zones and seamless chapters do, what "Only on Wi-Fi" waits for, why more parallel pages can be slower on some sites, and what clearing the cache or cookies removes and keeps.

## Where things moved

- Theme, Material You colours and **Language** are under **Appearance**.
- Library checks, categories and **Local files** are under **Library and updates**.
- Tracker accounts and automatic linking are under **Trackers**.
- Backups, Mihon import and automatic backups are under **Backup and restore**.

## Fixes

- **Android backup keeps your settings again.** In v0.6.1 the settings file was left out of cloud backup because it also held tracker sign-ins. Sign-ins and the automatic backup folder now live in a file of their own, which stays out of cloud backup; the settings file is backed up again.

## Updating

Install it over v0.6.1 as usual. On the first start Lume moves your tracker sign-ins to their new place; you stay signed in. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## For developers

- Settings live in `ui/screens/settings`: `SettingsScreen` (sections and search), one file per larger section, `SimpleSettingsPages` for the short ones, and `SettingsCatalog`, the list of searchable settings. A new setting needs a `SettingInfo` entry and a `SettingItem(id)` around its rows.
- `searchSettings` (`domain/SettingsSearch.kt`) ranks matches; it ignores case, accents and ё / е, and is unit-tested.
- `AppPreferences` keeps private values (tokens, app keys, the backup folder) in a second DataStore, `lume_private`, and moves them out of `lume_prefs` on start (Robolectric test). `data_extraction_rules.xml` and `backup_rules.xml` exclude `lume_private` instead of `lume_prefs`.
- New route `settings/{page}?focus={id}`.
