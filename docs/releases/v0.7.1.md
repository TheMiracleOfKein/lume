# Lume v0.7.1

Choose which titles download their new chapters by themselves, instead of the whole library or nothing.

## Highlights

- **Subscribe to a title.** A bell next to the bookmark on a library title's page. With it on, the library check downloads that title's new chapters as soon as it finds them.
- **Download new chapters: Off / All titles / Subscribed.** Settings → Downloads now has three choices instead of a switch:
  - **Off**: nothing downloads by itself;
  - **All titles**: every library title, as the old switch did (the bell is hidden then);
  - **Subscribed**: only titles with the bell on.
- **One tap to start.** Ringing the bell while downloads are off switches them to "Subscribed".
- Every title is still checked, and the notification still lists every title with new chapters. Only the downloading follows your choice.
- Subscriptions go into backups, sync to your other devices, and move with a title when you "Make main" another source.

## Updating

Install it over v0.7.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database gets one new column (an additive migration). Your old setting carries over: "Download new chapters" on becomes **All titles**, off stays **Off**.

## Known limits

- A device still on v0.7.0 syncs a title's settings without the subscription and can turn it off again. Update every synced device.
- Subscribing several titles at once from the library selection isn't there yet.

- Room 9 → 10: `title_prefs.autoDownload INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` (`MIGRATION_9_10`, schema `10.json`, `MigrationTest.migrate9To10…`). `TitlePrefsDto.autoDownload` defaults to false, so older backups and sync files still load.
- `LibraryUpdateWorker` asks `autoDownloadMode.downloads(titlePrefs.autoDownload)` before queueing.
- Build: AGP goes back from 9.4.1 to 9.2.1, the newest the installed Android Studio syncs. Dependabot ignores `com.android.*` 9.3 and newer, and lint's `AndroidGradlePluginVersion` check is off. Gradle 9.8.2 (new wrapper scripts and jar, checksum matches Gradle's), OkHttp 5.5.1, kotlinx-serialization 1.12.0, DataStore 1.3.0.
