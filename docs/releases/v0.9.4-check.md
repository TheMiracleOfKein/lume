## Changed areas (`git diff --stat v0.9.3..HEAD`, 4 files, 30 insertions, 3 deletions)

- `ReaderPage.kt`: in `ReaderPageImage`, the `onSuccess` check that marks a page as a tall candidate now calls `isTallAtWidth(...)` instead of comparing the decoded height with `MAX_DRAW_PX`.
- `TallPageTiles.kt`: new `isTallAtWidth(decodedWidth, decodedHeight, shownWidth)`. A page is tall when `decodedHeight * shownWidth / decodedWidth > 4096`. Judged by shape, because Coil shrinks a long strip to the height cap.
- `TallPageTilesTest.kt`: JVM test for the new function (not run here).
- `docs/ROADMAP.md`: wording only.

## Possible bug spots (from reading the code)

1. Tall pages that switch to slices (`TiledPage`): spinners per slice, gaps or seams between slices, black slices.
2. Scroll position jumping when a page switches from the single image to slices.
3. Pinch or double-tap zoom on a tall page (sharp detail overlay).
4. Normal-height pages must look unchanged (the new check should not trigger for them).
5. Paged (non-webtoon) reading modes unchanged.

## Findings

### Serious

None found. No crash, broken navigation, data loss or layout break reproduced. No tall-strip slice problems could be checked (see below), so the main purpose of this release is not verified on device.

### Minor and to check

- F1. In webtoon mode on Overgeared Chapter 0, some stretches show large black areas under panels with no spinner. They look like dark artwork, but they could be an unloaded slice or gap. Repro: Asura Scans, Overgeared, Chapter 0, switch to Webtoon, scroll through pages 2 to 8. Action: confirm on a strip page whether the black area is art (compare against the source page in a browser).
- F2. The emulator's adb scroll is coarse, so no scroll-jump test was made with precision. Repro needed: stop at a page boundary before a tall page, then check that the view does not move when the page switches to slices.

## Follow-up: page sizes from the image cache

The pages read in step 3 were pulled from the app's image cache afterwards and their WebP headers read. Overgeared Chapter 0 pages are 720 x 5345, 720 x 5073 and 720 x 3812 px. At the emulator's 1080 px width, the first two are about 8,000 px tall, so they are long strips: before this fix they were decoded at about 552 x 4096 and stretched to the screen width. With the fix they take the slice path (`tileSampleSize(720, 1080)` = 1, 5345 > 4096). The webtoon screenshots of these pages show clean edges with no stretching, no seams between slices, and no black slices. The black area in F1 is part of the artwork: a night-sky panel that fades into a glowing crystal further down. So F1 is not a bug.
