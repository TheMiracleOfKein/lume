# Lume v1.11.0

Cheaper AI. Translation, the recap and codex summaries send much less with each request and make fewer of them. One name stays one name, even with "-san" or "'s" on the end. A new meter shows what AI used this month.

## New

- **Used this month.** Settings → AI and translation shows, for each feature (translation, the recap with its chapter summaries, codex summaries), how many requests it made and how many tokens went out and came back. When the service reports what a request cost, the total cost shows too; OpenRouter does.
- **One name, one term.** AI translation now gives each name in its base form. "Yu San A-san" and "Yu San A's" become one suggestion, "Yu San A", which already covers the other forms in the text. This works for hyphenated Japanese and Korean honorifics (-san, -sama, -kun, -nim, -ssi and others, in Latin or Russian letters), honorifics written in kana or hangul, and possessives. Forms with a space ("Lin Yang") or hyphenated pinyin ("Ou-yang") are left alone, as they are usually part of the name.
- **Duplicates cleaned up.** Suggestions from earlier versions that repeat a name in another form are removed when the title or its glossary opens. If your own terms hold two forms of one name, the glossary shows them under **Same name, several forms** with **Merge**. Merge keeps the first form, and the others become other spellings of the name's codex entry.

## Changes

- **Smaller translation requests.** Each request carries only the glossary terms its text contains, not the whole glossary. With a long glossary, this was most of every request.
- **Fewer translation requests.** A chapter goes in parts of about 10,000 characters (was 6,000); the first part stays short, so the screen still fills fast. If a chapter fails part way, the parts already translated are kept, and **Retry** only asks for the rest.
- **Less waiting, less paying for "thinking".** Answers have a size cap, and models that think before answering are asked to think as little as possible: a translation gains little from it, and every thinking token is billed.
- **OpenRouter's Automatic is cheaper.** It now takes a cheap, fast model (DeepSeek V4 Flash, else Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite, DeepSeek V3.2 or Gemini 2.5 Flash). OpenRouter's own router, which can pick expensive models, is used only when none of them is offered.
- **OpenAI's newest models work.** GPT-5 models only accept their default settings; Lume no longer sends the ones they refuse. Any setting a service refuses by name is left out from then on, without failing the request.
- **A busy service is asked again** once, after the wait it asks for.
- **The recap makes fewer requests.** Chapters without a summary get theirs together, in one request. A summary in any language is used, so switching the translation language doesn't write them again. Opening "Previously…" again at the same spot shows the same text without a new request.
- **Codex summaries send less.** Summarize with AI sends the chapter summaries that mention the name, plus the last four, instead of up to thirty.
- **Accept all** in the glossary adds the codex entries in one go, and matches names in any of their forms.

## Updating

Install it over v1.10.0 as usual. Nothing in the database changes. Translated chapters you already have stay translated: nothing is translated again.

## Privacy

Nothing new is sent. Translation requests now carry fewer glossary terms. The usage totals are kept on the phone only and are not part of backups or sync. See [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md).

## For developers

- `LlmClient.chat` takes `maxTokens`, `feature` and `retries`. `chatBody` sets `max_tokens` (`max_completion_tokens` for OpenAI), reasoning effort per service, and OpenRouter's usage report. A 400 that names an optional parameter drops it for that base URL and model for the rest of the run (`refusedParams`). 429 and 502–504 are retried after `Retry-After`, at most 20 s.
- `LlmAnswer.usage` (`LlmUsage`) feeds `AiUsage`: monthly totals per `AiFeature` in the private store (`ai_usage`), three months kept.
- `llm/NameKey.kt`: `nameKey`, `baseName` and `isBaseName`. `filterSuggestions` works on keys; `translate/GlossaryTidy.kt` has `tidyGlossary`, `redundantSuggestions` and `confirmedDuplicates`.
- `LlmProvider` sends `"glossary": {"N": [source, target]}` with only the `data-g` numbers its HTML holds, plus session terms found in its text. `PROMPT_VERSION` is unchanged, so cached chapters stay valid. `ChapterTranslator` keeps finished parts in `<hash>.part.json`.
- `AiQueue.serial` runs a title's digest jobs one after another, two AI jobs at most at a time. `AiRecap.digests` batches missing digests (40,000 characters per request) and caches the recap in `cacheDir/ai-recap`. `StoryKnowledge.relevantDigests` picks the digests for a summary.
