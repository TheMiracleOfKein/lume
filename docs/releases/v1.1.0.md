# Lume v1.1.0

The Ranobe mode can now be used: import your EPUB and TXT books and read them in a new text reader. Online novel sources come next.

## New

- **Local books.** Settings → Library → **Import books…** picks EPUB and TXT files; the folder import and watched folders pick them up too. Each file becomes a book under the **Local books** source, with its cover, author, description and chapters.
  - EPUB 2 and EPUB 3: chapter names from the book's table of contents, pictures inside the text.
  - TXT: UTF-8, or Windows-1251 for older Russian files. Chapters are split on headings like "Chapter 12", "Глава 12", "Пролог" or "Epilogue"; a file without headings is split into parts.
- **Text reader.** A scrolling reader for novels:
  - tap the middle to show or hide the bars, tap the top or bottom of the screen to scroll a screen;
  - the next chapter follows on as you scroll; the chapter list and previous / next chapter are in the bottom bar;
  - settings: text size, line spacing, side margins, paragraph spacing, serif or sans-serif, justified text (with hyphenation), and App, Light, Sepia, Dark or Black theme.
- **Your place in a book** is kept to the paragraph, and the book shows in Continue reading and History like a manga does. Chapters you finish are marked as read, and reading time counts in Reading statistics. Incognito keeps nothing.

- **Each mode remembers its own source.** Home and Catalog in Ranobe show your books, and switching back to Manga brings back the manga source you had.

Only the full build has the Ranobe mode; the manga build is unchanged.

## Not yet

- Online novel sources (RanobeLib first), downloading novel chapters, and novel lists on trackers.

## Updating

Install it over v1.0.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads and sign-ins are kept. No database change.

## For developers

- One reader route: the shell opens the reader of the source's kind (the source, else the library entry, else the saved progress, else comics). Feature modules register readers with `ShellGraph.reader(kind)`.
- The source registry's wrappers forward `NovelSource.getChapterText`; `requireNovel()` sits next to `requireComic()`. The download queue refuses titles that are not comics.
- `:feature:novel` now has `NovelBlocks` (HTML to headings, paragraphs, pictures and breaks), `EpubBook`, `TxtBook`, `LocalNovelEngine` (`local-novel`), and the text reader (`NovelReaderViewModel`, `NovelReaderScreen`). `ContentModule.localBookExtensions` tells the importer which files are books.
- Reader settings are in `AppPreferences.novelReaderSettings`. The position is the block index and the fraction inside it, in the existing `pageIndex` / `pageOffset` columns; progress rows carry `mediaKind = "novel"`.
- New JVM tests for the parser, EPUB, TXT, the engine, the registry, the import naming, the reader view model and positions; screenshot tests for the reader and its settings. The device check is in [v1.1.0-check.md](v1.1.0-check.md).
