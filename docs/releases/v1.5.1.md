# Lume v1.5.1

Trackers and search that keep novels and manga apart, and a reader settings sheet that closes when you drag it down.

## Changed

- **Trackers per mode**: Ranobe mode lists only the trackers that keep novel lists: Shikimori, AniList and MyAnimeList. Kitsu and MangaUpdates have manga only, so they appear in Manga mode only. This applies to the Trackers button on a title and to Settings → Trackers. The Trackers dialog on a novel says which trackers it lists.
- **Search results say what they are**: every result in the Trackers dialog shows its format and year under the name, for example "Light novel · 2018" or "Manhwa · 2020". A novel and its manga often share the same name; now you can tell them apart before linking. A result the tracker marks as the other kind (a manhwa offered for a novel) is left out, and automatic linking skips it too.
- **Search follows the mode**: "All sources" search in Ranobe mode looks only at novel sources, and in Manga mode only at comics sources. Finding a title on other sources also stays with sources of the title's kind.

## Fixed

- AniList search results no longer show "null" as the name of an entry without an English title.
- An AniList novel search that finds nothing now says why: AniList has no entries for many Korean and Chinese web novels (Omniscient Reader, Solo Leveling). Shikimori has them.
- **The reader's settings sheet closes after a slow drag down.** Before, a slow drag could spring the sheet back up when the finger lifted, so it wouldn't close. The same applies to the chapter list and the bookmarks sheet in both readers.

## Planned

- Trackers made for novels: NovelUpdates, RanobeDB, and novels on MangaUpdates. See the roadmap.

## Updating

Install it over v1.5.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads, sign-ins and tracker links are kept. No database change.

## For developers

- `Tracker.kinds` says which `MediaKind`s a tracker serves; `TrackerRegistry.forKind(kind)` lists them. `TrackerSync.autoLink`, the Trackers dialog and Settings → Trackers use it.
- `TrackerSearchResult` gains `format: TitleFormat?` and `year: Int?`, read from AniList (`format`, `countryOfOrigin`, `startDate`), MyAnimeList (`media_type`, `start_date`), Shikimori (`kind`, `aired_on`), Kitsu (`subtype`, `startDate`) and MangaUpdates (`type`, `year`). `TrackerSearchResult.matchesKind` drops entries of the other kind.
- Global search and `TitleSourceRepository.searchableSources` filter sources by `manifest.kind`.
- `rememberEasyDismissSheetState()` (`core/ui`): a full-height `SheetState` with a 700 dp/s fling threshold instead of Material's 125 dp/s, so a slow drag past 56 dp closes the sheet even when the finger moves back a little as it lifts.
