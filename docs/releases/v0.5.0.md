# Lume v0.5.0

Search gets filters, more pages and a history. Downloads run in the background with a notification and can be paused, reordered and saved as CBZ. Backups can go anywhere and run on their own, and a Mihon or Tachiyomi backup moves your library to Lume in one step. MyAnimeList, Kitsu and MangaUpdates join Shikimori and AniList. The app follows the system theme, can use pure black or your wallpaper's colours, and has incognito mode and an app lock.

## Highlights

- **Filters in search.** The button next to the search field opens the source's filters: sort, status, audience, content rating and more on MangaDex; sort, status and adult content on Madara sites; sorting on the others. Filters alone are a search too, for example "every completed title, by rating".
- **Downloads in the background.** A notification shows what is downloading, with Pause and Cancel all. Downloads go on with the app closed and start again after Android stops it.
- **Move from Mihon or Tachiyomi.** Settings → Restore from a file reads a `.tachibk` backup: titles from sources Lume has, their categories, tracker links, read chapters and the last chapter you read. Titles from other sources are listed.
- **Three more trackers.** MyAnimeList, Kitsu and MangaUpdates. On every tracker you can now edit the status, score, chapters read and, where the tracker keeps them, the start and finish dates.

## Sources

- Search results load more pages as you scroll.
- **Recent searches** appear under the search field; tap one to search again.
- **Per-source settings** (Sources → ⋮ → Settings). MangaDex: chapter languages, content ratings and data saver.
- **Browse any source** without making it the active one (⋮ → Browse).
- **Pin** sources to the top of the list, or **disable** the ones you don't use: they leave global search.
- **Open links from the browser.** Share a title page to Lume, or open a link of a bundled source (MangaDex, MangaLib, ReManga, …) with Lume, and the title opens.
- **Pack catalogue.** Sources → Check packs and updates lists the source packs in `docs/packs/index.json`, installs them and updates installed ones.

## Downloads

- Pause and resume the queue, move a waiting chapter up, down or to the front, retry every failed download at once.
- Chapters from different sources download at the same time; pages of a chapter download 1 to 4 at once (Settings → Downloads).
- Every page is checked to really be an image. A site that answers with an error page or a check now fails the download instead of saving a broken page.
- **Save as CBZ:** one archive per chapter, with ComicInfo.xml, so other readers can open it too.
- **Storage:** save new downloads to internal storage, external storage or an SD card.
- **Download new chapters** found by library checks, and **delete chapters after reading** (when you close the reader).
- The Downloads screen shows how much space downloads use, per title and in total.

## Local files

- Settings → Local files → **Import a folder** copies a folder from your phone into the local library: a library of series, one series, or one chapter of images.
- Local titles use `cover.jpg`, `ComicInfo.xml` and Mihon's `details.json` for their cover and details. Titles made only of `.cbz` files get their first page as the cover.
- **Clean temporary files** removes unpacked archives.

## Backups

- **Save a backup to…** any folder or cloud drive, and **restore from a file** anywhere.
- **Automatic backups** every 1, 3 or 7 days, keeping the newest 3, 5 or 10, optionally copied to a folder you pick.
- Backups now include your settings. Account sign-ins stay out.

## Trackers

- **MyAnimeList** signs in through its website. **Kitsu** and **MangaUpdates** sign in with your username and password; Lume keeps only their sign-in token.
- **Edit** (Title → Trackers → Edit): status, score, chapters read, start and finish dates.
- **Shikimori stays signed in:** its token is renewed automatically.
- **Automatic linking:** when you add a title to the library, each signed-in tracker links it if it finds a title with exactly the same name. Settings → Link trackers automatically turns it off.

## App

- **Theme:** System, Light or Dark; **pure black** for OLED screens; **Material You** colours on Android 12 and newer.
- **Tablets and wide windows** get a navigation rail on the left instead of the bottom bar.
- **About and licenses** in the menu and in Settings.
- **Clear cache**, **clear cookies**, and **DNS over HTTPS** (Cloudflare, Google, AdGuard, Quad9) for providers that block sites through DNS.
- **Incognito mode** (menu or Settings): reading leaves no history, progress or read marks.
- **App lock** asks for the phone's screen lock when Lume opens, and again after 30 seconds away.
- **Hide in recent apps** blanks Lume's preview in the app switcher and blocks screenshots.
- Chapter labels that Russian sources send ("Том 1 Глава 5") are shown in the app language. Settings → General turns it off.

## Updating

Install it over v0.4.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database is unchanged.

## Known limits

- MyAnimeList needs a client ID in the build (`LUME_MAL_CLIENT_ID` in `local.properties`). Release builds without it show a message instead of the sign-in page.
- The download storage can be internal storage, external storage or an SD card, not any folder from the file picker.
- Local imports are copies; the original folder is not watched for changes.
- Read marks from a Mihon backup are matched by chapter number. Chapters without a number are not marked.

## For developers

- No database migration in this release (still version 8). New settings live in DataStore.
- New dependencies: `kotlinx-serialization-protobuf` (Mihon backups), `okhttp-dnsoverhttps`, `androidx.biometric`.
- `CatalogSource` gains `titleIdFromUrl()` (shared links and Mihon import) and `settings()` (per-source settings, merged into `engineConfig`). The filter model gained labels and copy-on-write helpers.
- Downloads run in `DownloadWorker` (a foreground WorkManager job, `dataSync` type).
- New pure logic with unit tests: source links and pack versions, filters, image signatures and ComicInfo.xml, Mihon backup parsing and source matching, tracker matching and dates, chapter label translation.
- The pack catalogue is `docs/packs/index.json`; pack URLs are relative to it.
- What comes next is in [docs/ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).
