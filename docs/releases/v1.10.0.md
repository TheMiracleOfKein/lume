# Lume v1.10.0

AI setup in one place: keys, checks and the active service and model live in Settings, the reader only picks. Translation says what it is doing, and the recap waits until you ask for it.

## New

- **Settings → AI and translation.** A new page in the main Settings list (Ranobe mode):
  - **Active AI service**: OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Gemini or **Own server**. Translation, the recap and codex summaries all use the active service and its model. Switching it here or in the reader switches it everywhere.
  - Each service shows whether its key works: **No key yet**, **Key saved, not checked yet**, **Key works · 312 models available · checked 5 minutes ago**, **The service refused this key**, **no credits left**, **No answer from the service**, or that the picked model isn't offered. Saving a key checks it at once, **Check now** checks it again, and a key whose last check is over a day old is checked when you open the page. For OpenRouter the check also looks at the key itself and its credits.
  - The saved key shows only its last four characters, with **Replace the key** and **Remove the key**, and a link to where the service gives keys.
  - **Model**: every service has **Automatic**. On OpenRouter, OpenRouter picks the model for each request. DeepSeek uses `deepseek-chat`. OpenAI and Gemini use a fast, inexpensive model from the service's own list, chosen when the key is checked. An own server uses the first model it lists. **Choose a model…** picks another one for that service; **Back to automatic** returns at any time.
  - **DeepL**: the key moved here too, and its check shows the characters used this month.
- **Translate sheet: pick only.** The reader's translation sheet has no key or address fields any more. For AI it shows the services that have a key and the model (Automatic, your pick, or **Other model…**), plus a button to the settings. DeepL shows its status and the same button.
- **Clearer translation.** While a chapter waits for its first answer, the top bar says **Waiting for OpenRouter… 12 s**, and the sheet says which service and model it was sent to. After 20 seconds it adds that big models can take a minute. Then it counts paragraphs. A failure names the service and the reason, with **Retry**, and with a way to the settings when the key, the credits or the model is the cause. **Stop translating** turns it off.
- **The recap is offered, not shown.** Coming back after a break, a small **Back after a break. Recall where you stopped?** bar appears with **Recap** and ✕. It goes away once you read on. The AI summary is only written when you tap **Recap**.
- **Recap any time.** A new ⟲ button in the reader's top bar shows "Previously…" for where you are now.

## Changes

- **Each reader opens in the original.** Translation no longer turns itself on when you open a title. If you leave a translated chapter and come back to the same title within 15 minutes, it stays on. Translated chapters are kept, so turning translation on again for a chapter you've read before is instant.

## Updating

Install it over v1.9.0 as usual. Nothing in the database changes. Your keys stay. The model you had picked stays with the service it was picked for; a service without a pick uses Automatic.

## Privacy

The key check sends only your key to its own service (the model list, OpenRouter's key endpoint, DeepL's usage endpoint), never chapter text. Check results are kept on the phone and are not part of backups or sync. See [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md).

## For developers

- `ShellGraph.settingsPage(key)` lets a content module supply a top-level settings page; `SettingsPage.Ai` (Ranobe mode) is the first.
- `AiServices` (`feature/novel/llm/`) resolves the active service and its model (per-service `ai_model_<service>` prefs, the old single `translate_llm_model` still counts for the service that was active) and runs key checks, stored as `ServiceCheck` JSON in the private store (`ai_check_<service>`, `ai_check_deepl`).
- `LlmPreset.autoModel(available)` picks the automatic model from a preference list with a close-match fallback.
- `ServiceHttpException` carries the HTTP code, so a refused key, missing credits or an unknown model can point to the settings.
- `TranslationProgress` gains `Waiting` (service and start time) and carries the service in `Working` and `Failed`. `TranslationSession` keeps the last translated title in memory for the 15-minute return.
