# Lume v0.7.7

Signing out of a Lib site now really signs you out, so you can log in with another account. Going back between screens looks softer.

## New

- **Sign-out ends the site session.** Signing out of MangaLib, HentaiLib or YaoiLib used to forget only Lume's copy of the login. The site still remembered you, so the login screen found the old account and closed at once. Sign-out now also clears the site's own session, and the next login shows the login form.
- **Saved passwords on the login screen.** Tap the e-mail field and Android offers the logins saved in Google Password Manager, the same ones Chrome uses. A short hint above the site says so.
- **Softer back animation.** When you go back (gesture, back button or the arrow), the screen you leave shrinks a little into a card with rounded corners and a soft shadow, and the screen underneath shows through instead of a hard edge sliding across. Moving forward and switching tabs look the same as before.

## Updating

Install it over v0.7.6 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. No database changes.

If the login screen still closes on its own after updating, sign out once more: the old site session from before this version is cleared then.

## Known limits

- "Sign in with Google" on the site may not work: Google blocks its sign-in inside apps' web views. Use e-mail and password (autofill fills them in).
- Signing out of one Lib site also ends the web session of the shared lib.social account, so the other Lib sites ask for a login the next time you open their login screen. Their saved Lume sign-ins are kept.
- Autofill needs an autofill service on the phone (Settings → Passwords & accounts). Google's is the default on most phones.

## For developers

- New `data/source/LibWebSession`: on sign-out, deletes the WebView storage and expires the cookies of the site's origin and `lib.social` / `auth.lib.social`, keeping `cf_clearance`, `__cf*` and DDoS-Guard cookies. JVM tests in `LibWebSessionTest`.
- New `ui/nav/BackGesture.kt`: `rememberBackGestureActive()` reads `NavigationEventDispatcher.transitionState` (navigationevent-compose 1.1.2, now a direct dependency) and replaces the internal `Transition.isSeeking` the removed v0.3 corners needed. `BackGestureCard` clips the leaving destination to the display corner radius with a shadow while a gesture is held or the entry is popping; `AppShell` records popping entries in `popExitTransition`.
- `Motion.screenPopExit` adds `scaleOut(0.9)`; `screenPopEnter` fades in from 0.5.
