# Lume v1.5.2

A reader settings sheet that stays still.

## Fixed

- **The reader's settings sheet no longer twitches.** After a slow drag, and sometimes with no touch at all, the text reader's settings sheet kept jumping up and down without end. The sheet was tall enough to reach the status bar; there it grew by the status bar's height, moved, shrank again and never settled. The catalogue filter sheet had the same problem and the same fix in v0.9.16. The sheet now stops a little below the status bar and scrolls inside when its content is taller. The same applies to the manga reader's settings, the text reader's chapter list and the manga reader's bookmarks, and now also to the library options sheet and the chapter filter sheet, which could hit the same problem. Every sheet in the app now shares one fix, so a new sheet can't miss it.

## Updating

Install it over v1.5.1 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads, sign-ins and tracker links are kept. No database change.

## For developers

- `LumeBottomSheet` (`core/ui`, `SheetStates.kt`) is the app's only bottom sheet: bottom-only window insets, and content capped at the window height minus the status bar (counted even while hidden), the bottom insets and 72 dp. All eight sheets use it; `FilterSheetInsets` is gone. Content taller than the cap scrolls; the chapter filter sheet gained `verticalScroll` for this.
- `BottomSheetRuleTest` (`core/ui`) scans `app`, `core` and `feature` sources and fails on any direct `ModalBottomSheet` call outside `SheetStates.kt`.
- Released without the emulator gate and the release smoke test, on the user's request.
