# Lume v1.5.2

A reader settings sheet that stays still.

## Fixed

- **The reader's settings sheet no longer twitches.** After a slow drag, and sometimes with no touch at all, the text reader's settings sheet kept jumping up and down without end. The sheet was tall enough to reach the status bar; there it grew by the status bar's height, moved, shrank again and never settled. The catalogue filter sheet had the same problem and the same fix in v0.9.16. The sheet now stops a little below the status bar and scrolls inside when its content is taller. The same applies to the manga reader's settings, the text reader's chapter list and the manga reader's bookmarks.

## Updating

Install it over v1.5.1 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads, sign-ins and tracker links are kept. No database change.

## For developers

- `ReaderSheetInsets` and `ReaderSheetContent` (`core/ui`, `SheetStates.kt`): reader sheets use bottom-only window insets and cap their content at the window height minus the status bar (counted even while hidden), the bottom insets and 72 dp, like `FilterSheetInsets` and the `FilterPanel` height cap.
- Released without the emulator gate and the release smoke test, on the user's request.
