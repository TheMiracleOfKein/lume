2. Import: Settings > Library and updates > "Import books…" (picker, multi-select), and "Import a folder from the device" / watched folders for EPUB and TXT files inside a folder.
3. Text reader (scrolling): tap middle toggles bars; tap top/bottom third scrolls a screen; bottom bar progress, previous/next chapter, chapter list, settings sheet (text size, line spacing, side margins, paragraph spacing, serif/sans, justify, App/Light/Sepia/Dark/Black).
4. Position saved per paragraph (History, Continue reading); next chapter appended near the end; one reader route picks comics or text reader by the source's kind.
5. Downloads hidden for novel titles. Mode switch (long press on the logo), Home, Library, Catalog and Sources are mode-aware.

## Problems found

### Serious

1. **Folder import of EPUB/TXT fails for every new book folder.** Steps: Settings > Library and updates > "Import a folder from the device" > Download > BookFolder > "Use this folder" > Allow. The result line at the bottom of the page reads: "Import failed: .../files/local-novel/Long Notes/Long Notes.txt.part: open failed: ENOENT (No such file or directory)". The book is not added. Cause: `LocalImporter.copyFile` writes `<name>.part` in `dest.parentFile` without creating that folder (core/data/src/main/java/com/lume/app/data/local/LocalImporter.kt, around line 234). `importBooks` does call `dest.parentFile?.mkdirs()` (line 144); the folder path does not. Watched folders use the same importer, so they are affected too.

2. **Mode switch leaves Home and Catalog on the previous mode's source.** Steps: full build, long press the logo, choose Ranobe. Home still shows the Asura Scans manhwa (New releases, Overgeared, Logging Out Is Too Hard ...), Catalog opens Asura "Popular" with manhwa rows, and tapping a row opens a comics title. The Menu at the same time says "Source: Local books". Reverse: choose Manga after using Ranobe; Home shows the Local books (Long Notes, Tower of the Lantern...) while the Menu says "Source: Asura Scans". Relaunching does not fix it; Home recovers only after a source is picked (Sources > More actions > Browse on Local books made Home show the novels). Cause: Home and Catalog use the global active source (`SourceRegistry.activeSource()`, core/data/src/main/java/com/lume/app/data/source/SourceRegistry.kt, line 136, used by core/ui/src/main/java/com/lume/app/ui/screens/BrowseViewModels.kt, `source()` at line 239), which is not filtered by the active mode. The Menu label falls back to the first source of the mode (core/ui/src/main/java/com/lume/app/ui/shell/MainMenu.kt, line 109). Same root cause as v1.0.0 Minor 2, now user-visible.

3. **"Open local titles" opens the comics "Local" source in Ranobe mode.** Steps: Settings > Library and updates > Local files > "Open local titles". It shows the empty comics Local source ("No titles here yet") while the three books exist. Cause: the row always navigates to `Routes.browse("local")` (core/ui/src/main/java/com/lume/app/ui/screens/settings/LibrarySettingsPage.kt, line 245), which is the comics source, not `local-novel`.

### Minor

1. **Reading progress shows 100% at the start of some chapters.** Opening the Tower "Copyright" chapter, the Russian "Пролог" and "Эпилог" showed "100%" at their first screen; the Chapter 5 jump showed 0%, and one screen down showed 16%. Needs a check of what the percent measures.
2. **Justified text keeps wide word gaps on some lines** even with hyphenation (for example "Morning found the tower exactly as" at 18 sp, and the preview line "counted the floors until the" in the settings sheet).
3. **Tower of the Lantern: the front matter is named "Chapter 1".** The "Copyright" page (not in the book's TOC) is the first chapter entry and is labelled "Chapter 1", the same as the real "Chapter 1: Floor 1". The list shows two "Chapter 1" rows, and the reader header says "Chapter 1" above "Copyright".
4. **Russian TXT chapter labels are English.** The chapter list shows "Ch. 1" ... "Ch. 15" although the book's headings are "Глава N". Preface, Пролог and Эпилог keep their names.
5. **Chapter headings appear twice in the reader** ("Chapter 1: Floor 1" as the app's chapter title, then the EPUB's own heading). Cosmetic; check whether it is intended.
6. **Folder import help text is comics-only.** Settings > Library and updates > "Import a folder from the device" still says "copied to /data/user/0/com.lume.app/files/local" and lists only comic formats; EPUB and TXT are not mentioned (they now do work through the folder import once Serious 1 is fixed).
7. **Import results appear only at the bottom of the Library settings page**, below "Scan now". After tapping "Import books…" or the folder row the user sees no feedback in place (the success text "Imported 3 books" and the failure text are off-screen until the page is scrolled).
