## Changed areas (`git diff v0.9.4..HEAD --stat`, 3 files, 50 insertions, 52 deletions)

- `ReaderPage.kt`: in `ReaderPageImage` the normal (lower quality) `AsyncImage` is always drawn now, and the tall-page slices (`TiledPage`) are drawn on top of it, instead of replacing it.
- `ReaderTallPage.kt`: slice bitmaps and the zoom-detail bitmap are converted to GPU hardware bitmaps after decoding (`decodeForDrawing`, falling back to a plain bitmap). A slice that is still decoding is now an empty `Spacer` (the spinner was removed), so the normal page shows through.
- `docs/ROADMAP.md`: wording only.

## Possible bug spots (from reading the code)

1. Slices drawing black or blank, or staying blank: the slice is now transparent while decoding and the page underneath must show.
2. Colour filter and e-ink mode not applied to slices (hardware bitmaps can't be drawn through a plain pixel path).
3. Double-tap zoom detail not sharp, or placed wrongly, after the hardware-bitmap change.
4. Normal-height pages changed by the always-drawn underlying image.
5. Paged modes changed.
6. Crashes or leaks when leaving the reader quickly (hardware bitmaps recycled while in use).
