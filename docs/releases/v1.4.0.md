# Lume v1.4.0

Ranobe mode gets its first English sources: Royal Road and Scribble Hub, the two biggest sites for original web fiction.

## New

- **Royal Road** and **Scribble Hub** in Ranobe mode (full and ranobe builds):
  - Catalog with popular and latest titles, search, title details and the whole chapter list;
  - chapters open in the text reader, scrolling or page by page, and download for offline reading like RanobeLib chapters;
  - links to a title or a chapter, opened from the browser or shared to Lume, open the title.
- Royal Road chapters you have to pay for (early access) aren't listed. The line the site hides in every chapter against copying is left out, and so are author's notes.
- Both sites sometimes show a Cloudflare check; Lume opens it in its browser, as for the manga sites that do the same.

## Updating

Install it over v1.3.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads and sign-ins are kept. No database change.

## Not yet

- Filters (tags, genres, sorting) for the two new sources.
- NovelBin and other sites with translated novels: they refuse plain requests from the places this was built in.
- Novel lists on trackers.

## For developers

- `RoyalRoadEngine` and `ScribbleHubEngine` in `feature/novel` (engine ids `royalroad`, `scribblehub`, one-site engines), with manifests in the novel module's assets, so the manga build has neither. `NovelHtml` cleans a chapter element for the reader (scripts, ads and attributes dropped, `data-src` pictures made absolute) and reads the classes a page's own styles hide.
- Royal Road's chapters come from the title page's `window.chapters` script; Scribble Hub's from `admin-ajax.php` (`wi_getreleases_pagination`). Details in [ENGINES.md](../ENGINES.md#royal-road-and-scribble-hub).
- Parser tests on pages saved from both sites (`feature/novel/src/test/resources/fixtures`), and `LiveEnglishNovelsTest` in the weekly live check (a Cloudflare check counts as skipped).
- Released without a device check: checked with the parser tests and a live run of Royal Road from a desktop. Scribble Hub's full chapter list (`admin-ajax.php`) hasn't been seen on a device yet; the items are in [DEVICE_TESTING.md](../DEVICE_TESTING.md#v140-royal-road-and-scribble-hub).
