# Lume v1.15.0

Reading comfort in the novel reader: things that long reading sessions in popular ranobe readers rely on.

## New

- **Hide the chapter title in the text.** "Chapter title in the text" in the reader settings hides the chapter number and title above the text, including a title the chapter's own text starts with. The top bar still shows it, and the reading position stays where it was.
- **Brightness on the left edge.** Swipe up or down along the left edge of the screen to make it brighter or dimmer; the level shows while you swipe and is kept for the next time. **Use system brightness** goes back to the phone's level. Leaving the reader gives the screen back its normal brightness. On by default; it can be turned off.
- **Volume keys turn pages** (off by default): volume down goes forward, volume up back, in scroll and page modes. The volume itself doesn't change.
- **Status line**: while the bars are hidden, a small line under the text shows the time, the chapter progress and the battery.
- **Paragraph indent**: off, 1 em or 2 em for the first line of each paragraph.
- **Auto-scroll** in scroll mode: ▶ on the bottom bar, with a speed from 1 to 10 in the settings. A tap in the middle pauses it, a drag stops it, and it carries on into the next chapter.

All the new options are also in Settings › Reader (Ranobe mode) and in settings search.

## Details

- The new settings are shared by every title; "Save for this title" still covers text size, theme and page mode.
- Read aloud still speaks the chapter title when it is hidden in the text.
- The brightness swipe takes only a vertical drag that starts on the edge; a tap there still scrolls or turns the page.
