# Lume v0.9.5

Smoother short swipes on long webtoon pages.

## Fixed

- **Long pages don't jump when you start moving them.** On pages made of one long image, a short swipe could start with a jerk, while long swipes were smooth. The sharp slices of such pages now stay in the phone's graphics memory, so the first moment of a drag no longer waits for them to be loaded there again.
- **No blank flash while a long page sharpens.** Until its sharp slices are ready, a long page keeps showing the normal picture instead of a blank area with spinners.

## Updating

Install it over v0.9.4 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks and downloads are kept. The database doesn't change.

## For developers

- `ReaderTallPage.kt`: slices (`TileImage`) and the zoom detail (`PageDetail`) go through `decodeForDrawing`, which copies the region decoded by `BitmapRegionDecoder` into a `Bitmap.Config.HARDWARE` bitmap and recycles the plain one. A plain bitmap is uploaded to the GPU on its first draw, and again whenever HWUI drops the texture while the page is still. That upload landed in the first frame of the next drag. It falls back to the plain bitmap if the copy fails.
- `ReaderPage.kt`: the Coil `AsyncImage` stays composed under `TiledPage`. Slices that are still decoding, or failed, are transparent (the per-slice spinner is gone).
- On the emulator the jump can't be reproduced: its host GPU uploads in about 0 ms. A frame-by-frame layout log showed no change in item offsets or sizes at drag start, which points to drawing, not layout.
