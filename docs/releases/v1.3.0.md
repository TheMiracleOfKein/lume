# Lume v1.3.0

The text reader can turn pages, novel downloads go to a nearby phone like manga ones, and there is a third build with the Ranobe mode only.

## New

- **Page by page.** A new switch in the text reader's settings (and in Settings → Reader) turns the text into pages:
  - swipe sideways, or tap the right or left third of the screen, to turn a page; the middle still shows the bars;
  - a paragraph goes on over the next page, and every chapter starts on a new one; a picture gets a page of its own;
  - your place is kept the same way as in scroll mode, so switching between the two, changing the text size or rotating the phone keeps it.
- **Novel downloads to a nearby phone.** Downloaded novel chapters can now be sent with **Send to a nearby phone**, with their pictures, like manga chapters. The other phone needs Lume 1.3.0. An older version still receives every manga chapter of the same transfer.
- **Ranobe build.** Releases now have a third APK, `lume-v1.3.0-ranobe.apk`, with the Ranobe mode only and none of the manga code. Like the manga build it installs over the others and keeps your data, and the in-app updater stays on it.

## Updating

Install it over v1.2.0 as usual. Your library, progress, bookmarks, downloads and sign-ins are kept. No database change.

The full and manga builds stay as they are. To try the ranobe build, install `lume-v1.3.0-ranobe.apk` over the one you have. Manga titles stay in the database and come back when you go back to the full build.

## Not yet

- Novel lists on trackers and English novel sites.

## For developers

- Nearby transfer: `OfferTitle.text` marks a text title, and `Control.Answer.text` says the receiver takes text chapters. Senders leave text chapters out for receivers that don't say so. A text chapter's files are `chapter.html` and then its pictures. The receiver stores them with `DownloadStore.saveReceivedTextChapter`, and `TextChapterFiles.receivedHtml` keeps only the chapter's own pictures and web links.
- Paged reader: `NovelPages.kt` (pure `paginate`, `pageOf`, line/fraction mapping, JVM-tested) and `NovelPagedText.kt` (a `HorizontalPager` with per-row text measurement, cached per layout). The setting is `NovelReaderSettings.paged` (DataStore `novel_paged`). The body style is shared through `novelBodyStyle`.
- Ranobe flavor: the feature modules are flavor dependencies, Coil components come from `InstalledModules.addImageComponents` (PDF pages only where comics ship), and each flavor's manifest lists the title links of its own sources. CI and the Release workflow build, check and publish the ranobe APK.
- The device check is in [v1.3.0-check.md](checks/v1.3.0-check.md).
