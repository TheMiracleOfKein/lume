# Code of conduct

Lume is a small project. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, including in disagreement;
- keep criticism about the work, not the person;
- assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise;
- not harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately;
- not post other people's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads and block people who don't follow these rules.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/TheMiracleOfKein).
